# Challenge the proposed reading journey

A proposed tree is useful because people can react to something concrete. Review
the reading promises and boundaries before investing in the articles themselves.
A plan cannot establish that unwritten prose will teach well.

For the relational collection, independent editorial, practitioner, learning-design
and fresh-reader perspectives supplied different evidence. An editor asked which
leaves could sustain an argument; a practitioner looked for usable entrances; a
learning reviewer examined prerequisites and transfer; the unbriefed reader tried
to navigate by the titles alone. These are useful possibilities when commissioning
subagents, not a fixed team for every planning decision.

Give informed reviewers the goals and relevant context. Preserve a fresh reading
of the proposal before explaining its rationale or sharing other findings. A reader
who already knows why two titles differ cannot show whether the titles communicate
that difference unaided. The style skill's context-free reference supports that role
without disclosing article histories.

The collaboration tool normally inherits the conversation when it forks an agent.
Use `fork_turns="none"` for the fresh reader and supply only the proposal, audience
and context-free voice reference. A role label alone does not remove inherited context.

## What the relational reviews changed

The editor favoured combining index mechanism and index choice, while the fresh
reader could distinguish their promises. We retained both outcomes in the map and
left the eventual article split open to what their worked explanations needed.
Similar subject matter did not by itself settle the boundary.

Two titles about waiting were harder to distinguish. Diagnosing a stall and handling
a transaction's outcome needed clearer entrances. This was a routing problem with
a reader consequence, rather than simply a preference for different wording.

The reviewers proposed different first batches. Those were arguments about useful
journeys, not replacement commissions. A suggested transfer exercise also did not
become a compulsory feature of the article. We could test whether understanding
transferred without printing the test itself in the published explanation.

The initial plan still missed something important. It proposed following stored
facts through a query and purchase; the user wanted an introduction for someone
who had never encountered a relational database. The revised brief established the
system, its representation and the purposes of its mechanisms before relying on a
worked operation. Thoughtful review had not guaranteed the correct content job.

See `docs/reviews/relational-tree-2026-10-04/` and
`docs/relational-collection-plan.md` for the proposal and decisions in context.
Preserve disagreements that explain a choice, and update a plan when better
understanding changes it.

## An actionable handoff

A useful planning record shows the proposed coverage, likely reader journeys,
important boundaries and the bounded work currently commissioned. It makes clear
what exists, what is only planned and what remains undecided. Choose the amount of
detail that lets an author start confidently without prescribing the argument.

The collection's full ambition, the next production slice and the visible navigation
may differ. Keep that distinction intelligible to the user and subsequent agents.
Planning reviews inform those choices; they do not authorise producing or publishing
the whole collection.
