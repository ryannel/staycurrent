Putting data near its users can reduce read latency. Keeping enough independent copies can survive a failed region. Keeping writes correct can require messages between those regions. A regional design has to choose all three together.

A locality label says where a row or table prefers to live. A survival goal says which failures must leave the required authority available. Neither alone proves where every copy, log, backup and metadata record resides.

Tenant t4 runs workshops near region A; tenant t9 is near region B. Their ordinary bookings are tenant-local. We will place the replicas for t4's session s7, remove a region, then add a global quota whose owner changes the write path. The replica counts and round-trip times below are explicit teaching assumptions, not a product's default placement.

## Count votes in failure domains

Consider a fixed three-voter replica group. A majority is two. If two voters are in A and one in B, a leader in A can obtain a local majority quickly. Losing B leaves two votes. Losing A leaves only one, so the remaining server cannot safely accept conflicting writes under this majority protocol.

Put one voter in each of A, B and C instead. Losing any one region leaves two. Every healthy write now needs an inter-region acknowledgement because no region alone has a majority.

Count the failure you actually intend to survive. Three nodes in different zones of one region can survive some node or zone failures yet all disappear in a region failure. Five replicas whose majority shares one power or account boundary can fail together even if their host names differ.

Quorum safety is per required group. Keeping the s7 data group available is insufficient if a routing, transaction-status or system metadata group loses its authority. Include those groups in a deployment survival test.

## Placement sets a network floor

Suppose local round trips within A take 1 ms, A–B takes 40 ms and A–C takes 90 ms. Ignore processing and client distance for this model. With voters A:2/B:1, a local leader can obtain two votes within A. With A:1/B:1/C:1, its fastest majority includes B and therefore pays a remote path. The five-voter 2/2/1 group also needs at least one remote voter for its three-vote majority.

Actual commit latency also includes the client's route, reads, concurrency control, durability and the product's commit protocol. Exchanges may overlap. Do not simply multiply “two-phase” by a region RTT and call it a benchmark. The picture gives a lower-bound dependency to check in traces and native tests.

Place request processing near the relevant write authority when possible. Sending an A-local booking through a gateway in C can add distance before the database's own coordination begins. A nearby read replica can help a historical display while leaving the write's quorum path unchanged.

## Local rows and a global rule

Initially r81 checks t4's s7 capacity, creates its booking and records o81 with a historical agreed amount. Colocating those records near A keeps its ordinary transaction focused on that locality, subject to the replica-survival contract.

Now the business adds a global quota: all tenants together may confirm at most 100 subsidised seats. Every subsidised booking must decrement that quota. If its authority is near C, the A-local purchase now reaches both the local session and the global quota group.

A globally hot quota is also a serial resource. Adding regions can increase distance to it without creating independent capacity. Regional allocations can trade immediate global flexibility for local progress: allocate A twenty subsidy rights, B thirty and retain the remainder elsewhere. The sum must never exceed 100, and transfers, stranded rights and recovery need a maintained protocol.

If the service requires one current global decision on every booking, accept that coordination path and budget it. A locality setting cannot make an inherently global invariant local by itself.

## Decide degraded reads separately

After a region failure, a local replica may have an earlier safe snapshot even when it cannot accept writes. Serving a historical catalogue can preserve a useful read experience if the interface labels and tolerates its age. Approving a booking from that view would need current authority.

A strongly current read can also depend on leadership or quorum under the implementation's protocol. A read-only replica with data is not automatically authorised to claim that no newer conflicting write exists. [Time and distributed reads](/learn/databases/relational/distributed-reads/) explains the difference between a safe past and a current answer.

Plan client routing after failure. New gateways, cold caches and longer surviving quorum paths can raise latency even though the promised failure is survived correctly. Survival is not a promise that performance stays at the healthy local baseline.

## Locality is one part of residency

A placement rule for primary rows is not a complete account of where information exists. Secondary indexes can repeat sensitive values. Replicas, temporary spill files, logs, backups, exports, change feeds, metadata and support diagnostics can all contain related information.

Define the data boundary and inspect how the selected product and service enforce it during normal operation, repair, rebalance and recovery. “Home region” can describe a performance preference; “restricted placement” may be a stronger but differently scoped control. Do not infer a complete organisational or contractual residency assurance from one table label.

Keys matter too. Encryption can protect copied bytes while its key service lives elsewhere. Recovery depends on access to those keys after the region or account fails. Keep residency, recoverability and privilege decisions connected rather than treating encryption as a placement substitute.

## Product survival mechanisms differ

CockroachDB's multi-region configuration separates table locality from zone/region survival goals and derives replica placement from those choices. Its regional-by-row and global table designs change the paths differently; system ranges remain part of availability. [CockroachDB locality and region survival](/learn/databases/relational/cockroachdb/locality/) develops the concrete configuration.

Spanner distinguishes voting read-write replicas, read-only replicas and witness roles under instance configurations. A read-only nearby copy is not equivalent to another voting full replica. Leader placement, available configurations and service-managed membership affect the contract.

A PostgreSQL primary with cross-region standbys, InnoDB replication and SQL Server availability groups use different acknowledgement and failover designs. Their regions do not automatically form the majority group drawn here. Synchronous waits, candidate selection and old-primary exclusion must be evaluated under that deployment. Embedded SQLite has no built-in multi-region authority protocol; an added service owns it.

The arithmetic model can check a proposed layout's necessary quorum property. It cannot prove that a product permits that exact placement, that every system group follows it or that its controller meets a failover-time objective.

## Rehearse the whole-region case

[The executable model](/learn/databases/relational/evidence/rl105-109-models.mjs) removes each region from each declared voter layout and checks the remaining majority. [Recorded results](/learn/databases/relational/evidence/rl108-model-observed.json) show every count and outcome. No network region or cloud failure was measured.

A native/service rehearsal should hold an identified booking through the failure, verify its outcome, check which reads and writes remain permitted, and measure client recovery. Repeat while a global quota transaction is active and while the cluster is already repairing a replica. Restore traffic under the surviving resource budget, then verify that repair re-establishes the intended placement.

The defensible design names its tolerated failure, required authority, healthy and degraded paths, and all retained copies. Geography changes those obligations even when the SQL schema stays the same.
