## Synthesis

- Cockroach Labs' founding in New York in 2015 by three former Google engineers, and the move to the Business Source License in 2019, are from general knowledge rather than a fetched page.
- The deprecation of interleaved tables in v20.2 is from memory of the release notes; the removal in v21.2 is supported only by the grammar comparison above.
- The read-path description of an intent encounter (check the record, queue if pending) combines the transaction layer's conflict and TxnWaitQueue sections into one narrative.
- The write-path diagram and numbered steps combine the transaction, distribution, and replication layer pages and the life-of-a-transaction page into a single sequence; the ordering of latch, lock table, and timestamp cache checks follows those pages.
- The claim that a secondary index write is a distributed transaction across as many ranges as there are indexes follows from the key encoding and range model; the docs state the cost more generally as "new values have to be written for both the table and the index".
- The capacity worked example (6 nodes of 16 vCPUs for 2 TiB and 30,000 QPS) applies the documented per-vCPU ratios and the 4-active-connections-per-vCPU rule; it's a first cut, not a documented result.
- The multi-region payments design in the interview section applies the survival goal and locality rules; the choice of three specific regions and the super-region caveat are judgement.
- The statement that CockroachDB has no columnar engine and that changefeeds are the route to analytics combines the FAQ's OLAP statement with the changefeed export documentation.
- The recommendation that every serialisable application needs a retry loop restates the docs' guidance as a hard rule; the docs phrase it as "should include client-side retry handling".
- The "search results in late September 2026 suggest the plans are being reshaped again" line about Cloud pricing rests on third-party indexed pages, not a Cockroach Labs source, which is why no rates are quoted.
- Interview answers throughout are my own framing of the documented mechanisms.
