Study the finished figures in [Sequence diagrams](../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) in their explanations. Imitate their teaching and visual standard. Let them lead choices of form, detail and placement for what this reader is learning.

Use the collection's [example facts](../staycurrent-continuity/SKILL.md), selecting what the explanation needs. Keep shared data and outcomes in one place. Build with existing article components and site colour tokens. For stepped figures, use the shared player and [implementation guide](references/stepper.md).

Judge the rendered figure in the article against the references. Inspect it at 375 px and desktop, in light and dark; exercise every control and option, including keyboard use. Keep controls understandable with their changing state. Provide a text alternative for interactive content. Text contrast needs 4.5:1; large text and graphics need 3:1. Coordinate checks with the preview/build owner and record limits honestly.

Distinguish illustrations from observed system behaviour. For database claims requiring execution evidence, follow [database verification](references/database-verification.md).
