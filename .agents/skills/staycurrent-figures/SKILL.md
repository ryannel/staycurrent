Study [Sequence diagrams](../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro). Choose the passage closest to what this reader is learning, including its working figure. Develop the explanation and figure together by adapting how that passage teaches. Compare them side by side: does your version make the idea as easy to understand?

Use the collection's [example facts](../staycurrent-continuity/SKILL.md), selecting what the explanation needs. Keep shared data and outcomes in one place. Use site colour tokens. For stepped figures, use the shared player and [implementation guide](references/stepper.md).

Judge the rendered figure in the article against the references. Inspect it at 375 px and desktop, in light and dark; exercise every control and option, including keyboard use. Keep controls understandable with their changing state. Provide a text alternative for interactive content. Text contrast needs 4.5:1; large text and graphics need 3:1. Coordinate checks with the preview/build owner and record limits honestly.

Distinguish illustrations from observed system behaviour. For database claims requiring execution evidence, follow [database verification](references/database-verification.md).
