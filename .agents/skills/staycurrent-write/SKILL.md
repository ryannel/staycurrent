description: Write or substantially revise an article by imitating the three fixed reference articles in its writing and teaching.
---

Read the complete reference text with `python3 scripts/read-writing-reference.py`: it lists numbered, bounded parts. Read each part separately, checking its END marker; recover any clipped output before designing. Then study [Sequence diagrams](../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) as complete finished articles. Follow their drawings through the explanations they develop before designing your own. Write an article that could sit beside them and feel written by the same author, adapting their teaching to what this reader already understands.

Use the plan to understand this article’s contribution and its neighbours. The collection carries the newcomer towards expert practice; this article must teach its own subject well. Let each section develop a subject or question the reader now has reason to understand, choosing its scope and depth from the explanation. Follow the references in how they name articles and sections. Prefer established, searchable industry terms in titles and headings where they fit naturally. Reshape the plan and leave working depth to relevant children, introducing and linking them where useful. Follow the collection's [example record and continuity](../staycurrent-continuity/SKILL.md); propose extensions centrally.

Use [staycurrent-figures](../staycurrent-figures/SKILL.md) for figures, tables, code and interactive tools. Coordinate rendered checks with the designated preview/build owner.

Check consequential claims against primary sources; list them under Sources and further reading. Let the fixed references lead the teaching. Distinguish illustrative models from observed results; keep applicable execution evidence with the work.

Reread the finished piece against the references, including captions and controls, and run the continuity pass. Return it for the commissioned review; standalone complete articles arrange [article review](../staycurrent-review/SKILL.md).
