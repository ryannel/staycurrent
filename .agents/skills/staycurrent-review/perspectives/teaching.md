# Teaching perspective

**Inputs:** pieces; relevant passages and finished figures from [Sequence diagrams](../../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) to calibrate the judgement; available parent, sibling and child plans; commission. Reviewers need not repeat the writer's complete-reference preparation.

## Checks

1. **Starting point.** Dependencies precede ideas; terms are explained at first use.
2. **Order.** Ideas build on earlier ones; the hardest part gets the most help.
3. **Examples.** A concrete running example carries both easy and awkward cases.
4. **Showing.** Can the intended reader learn the main ideas by following the illustration sequence, as in the references? Identify missing connections they must supply. In breakdown/tree modes, briefs identify what needs making visible.
5. **Judgement.** The reader can make the intended decision and knows when it changes.
6. **Gradual exposure.** The parent answers at high level before handing deeper mechanisms to children where wanted.
7. **Placement of additions.** For coordinator-named added passages/figures, say whether each improves the explanation, belongs here or with an existing owner, and sits where the argument needs it or reads as attached. Length alone is not a finding; needed additions may be as long as the teaching requires.

In tree mode, judge planned progression. In corpus mode, follow finished routes from the introduction, checking idea order along each route.
