# Reader lens

For the first review of a new or substantially rewritten article, complete the
[first reading](first-reading.md) and return its observations before using this file.
Then assess the whole article against the writing references. Retain the original
observations unchanged beside these informed findings. This is the second stage of
the same reader assignment; visual review belongs to a separate fresh reader.

**Inputs:** the finished article, the audience, and the
[voice guide](../../staycurrent-write/references/voice.md). Read relevant passages from
its current writing references to calibrate the judgement; a review need not repeat the
writer's full reference preparation. Do not read plans, author notes or earlier verdicts
on an initial article review. For a prose-only commission, use the supplied prose.

## The prose

1. **Openings and endings.** Read them beside the writing references. The opening gives
   enough context to understand the question and why it matters. The ending follows from
   the explanation without an added slogan, moral or needless recap. Learn the craft
   without demanding the references' jokes or dramatic premises.
2. **Plain, varied sentences.** The prose needs no decoding, and its rhythm varies rather
   than marching in uniform short sentences. Prefer literal wording; flag decorative
   metaphors, staged reveals or reversals that make the reader work without adding meaning.
   Short sentences can still leave gaps between them.
3. **Terms.** Each term is explained at first use and used consistently. Headings use the
   field's own terms.
4. **Confidence.** Flag repeated disclaimers, defensive negation, recaps of running-example
   facts that teach nothing here, and deferrals ("X owns that") where the reader needed
   an answer. Name the passages, and estimate how often each occurs.
5. **One piece.** The article reads as if written in one go, with no patch-like asides or
   stacked qualifications.
6. **Prose stands alone.** Read the whole article with every figure, caption and control
   omitted. Report missing premises, unexplained results and passages that narrate a
   figure. Removing figure references alone does not establish this.

For each section, say what useful understanding would be lost if it were cut. Judge the
effort of the whole article even when every sentence is clear. Keep the passages that
work, and distinguish a hard step needing more explanation from unnecessary explanation.
The writing should be generous towards readers and people making different choices.

## A collection

For an assembled collection, replace the per-article checks with these:

- Follow each reading route from the introduction. Ideas arrive before they are used, and
  each article opens from where the route leaves the reader.
- Terms, names and running-example facts are consistent across articles.
- No lesson is taught in full twice; each has one owner and the others point to it.

Rely on the article reviews for individual passages; the visual lens owns figure
consistency across the collection.

Collection checks and scoped repair confirmations do not restart the first-reading
stage. A substantially rewritten argument can require a new fresh reading under the
review skill's rules for widening a check.

Report whether you read built prose or an unrendered draft.
