name: staycurrent-review
description: Coordinate independent reviews of a breakdown, article tree, written article or corpus, or perform an assigned review perspective.
---

Review against the goal of taking an intelligent newcomer to expert practitioner with the least reader time and effort:

- **Breakdown:** two independent subagents, a practitioner for coverage and depth and a reader/editor for learning value and effort.
- **Tree or corpus:** three independent subagents, checking completeness and depth; structure and overlap; and teaching and flow.
- **Article:** two independent subagents, a practitioner for correctness, coverage and practical judgement and a writing/teaching reviewer for prose, animations and interaction.

Teaching and style reviewers read [Sequence diagrams](../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) in full, including their figures, and judge how effectively the work follows their writing and teaching. Return concrete findings with locations, send repairs to the responsible worker and recheck them; if assigned one perspective, perform that review directly without spawning another team.
