name: staycurrent-review
description: Review articles and collections for technical accuracy, teaching, writing and usable illustrations; confirm repairs in proportion to what changed.
---

Judge the reader's understanding and practical judgement against the commission. Use independent readers for the first full review; reviewers report problems without editing. The coordinator owns acceptance, repair scope and completion. Acceptance rests on the review's concrete examples and reasoning against the references; coverage establishes what was checked.

## Review once

Review each complete article for technical accuracy, teaching and writing, and rendered behaviour. Cover the relevant checks in [practitioner](perspectives/practitioner.md), [teaching](perspectives/teaching.md), [editorial](perspectives/editorial.md) and [visual](perspectives/visual.md). Teaching and editorial may share a reader. Technical and reader reviews may run in parallel; use the finished rendering for visual judgement and controls. Do not add an automatic final reading after this coverage.

Review an assembled collection once for gaps, ownership, duplication, prerequisite order and actual reading routes, using the [value](perspectives/value.md), practitioner and teaching checks. Reuse article reviews instead of reviewing every article again from every perspective. For a planned tree, review the same learning journey before writing; do not separately repeat every node's review. Plans need no prose or rendered-media review.

Give reviewers the audience, fixed version and inputs their judgement needs. Technical reviewers receive primary sources and applicable execution evidence; teaching receives relevant plans and example facts. A first editorial reading receives the finished piece and audience, withholding plans, author notes and earlier verdicts. Consult the fixed reference passages and finished figures needed to calibrate the review; reviewing is not another writer's complete-reference preparation.

## Repair and confirm

Consolidate findings before repair. Describe the reader's problem and desired understanding, not replacement wording. Use [staycurrent-revise](../staycurrent-revise/SKILL.md) for prose changes.

Confirm each accepted repair in its surrounding explanation and affected dependencies, including later uses. The original checker may confirm it; a coordinator may check a bounded correction against the relevant evidence. These are informed checks, not new blind reviews. Keep earlier checks unless the change invalidates their judgement. A new version label alone invalidates nothing.

Widen only for a concrete consequence: a changed argument or prerequisite order needs a wider reader assessment; changed claims, examples or mechanisms need technical checking; changed media or shared layout needs current rendered checks wherever affected. A fresh blind reading is useful for a substantially rewritten argument or an unresolved quality concern, not as a routine delivery gate. Do not start another collection-wide round after local repairs.

Reuse execution evidence while the relevant code, data, environment and verifier still apply; otherwise rerun the affected check or state its limit. Reused evidence does not replace inspection of changed rendered content. Keep mechanical provenance separate from author explanations and quality verdicts.

## Findings and completion

Report the version, scope, findings and material limits concisely. For a finding, give its location, concrete reader effect and supporting observation or source. A short coverage statement is enough for checks that pass; do not reproduce a large pass matrix by default.

- **Critical:** false or unsupported understanding, an incorrect action, an impossible argument, or a contradictory figure.
- **Medium:** the reader must stall, reread or supply missing understanding; an unnecessary repeated lesson also costs the reader.
- **Low:** optional polish that makes reading a little easier.

Severity describes the reader's problem, not the size of the review to repeat. The coordinator resolves disagreements against the commission and records disputed decisions. Repair and confirm every accepted substantive problem; do not downgrade it to finish. Optional polish does not reopen delivery.

Finish when accepted substantive findings are resolved, applicable checks cover the delivered work, and the coordinator judges it fulfils its promise coherently. Record outstanding optional work and evidence limits. Completion does not itself authorise publication.
