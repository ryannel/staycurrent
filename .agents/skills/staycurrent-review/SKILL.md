# Review

Judge whether the reader ends up able to do what the commission promised, at the least
cost in time and effort. Reviewers report problems; they do not edit. The coordinator,
meaning whoever assigned the review, accepts findings, arranges repairs and decides when
the work is done.

Look both for understanding the reader needed but did not get, and for time the article
took without giving anything back. Keep the original readings and disagreements visible.

## Lenses

A new or substantially rewritten article gets all four lenses, each from a separate,
fresh agent. When you are assigned one lens, load only the instructions for its current stage.

- [Teach-back](lenses/teach-back.md): can a reader with only the article answer its exit
  questions?
- [Senior practitioner](lenses/practitioner.md): is it correct, complete at expert level,
  and better than the best alternative a reader could find?
- [Reader](lenses/reader.md): does it read easily and does the prose stand alone?
- [Visual](lenses/first-visual-reading.md): can a reader understand each figure and learn the main
  explanation from their sequence, and do the figures render and work correctly?

A **plan** gets the practitioner lens on its dossier, tree and exit questions: coverage,
ownership, depth and page count. A **collection** gets the practitioner lens and the
collection checks in the reader and visual lenses, across its reading routes. It reuses
the article reviews rather than repeating them. An article with no exit questions cannot be taught
back: report that to the coordinator, who has them written first.

## Inputs

Give each reviewer the audience, the commit or build being reviewed, and only the inputs
its lens lists. Start agents without the author's conversation. The reader lens begins
with only prose, audience and [first-reading instructions](lenses/first-reading.md).
Use built HTML or text extracted from the rendering, without source comments or imports;
for a prose-only draft, use the supplied prose and state that rendered checks are pending.
Do not give this reader plans, exit questions, voice guidance, author notes or earlier
verdicts. Save its unaided observations before sending the voice guide and
the remaining [reader-lens checks](lenses/reader.md). These are two stages of the same
lens, not another reviewer. Teach-back retains its separate question-led test.

The visual reviewer is a different fresh reader. Initially give it only the isolated
figures, audience and [first visual reading](lenses/first-visual-reading.md). Save its
account before supplying the article, learning goals and informed visual checks.
Someone who has read the article cannot establish independent visual comprehension.

## Findings

For each finding, give its location, the reader's problem and the evidence: a passage, a
source or an observation. Keep observations separate from proposed repairs. Name useful
passages a repair should preserve. Report passing checks in a line.

- **Critical:** false or unsupported understanding, an incorrect action, an impossible
  argument, or a figure that contradicts the text.
- **Medium:** the reader must stall, reread or supply missing understanding, or cannot
  answer an exit question. Missing expert-level content and unnecessary repeated
  teaching belong here too. An unclear visual cause or a missing major lesson in the
  visual sequence is substantive, even if the prose supplies it.
- **Low:** optional polish.

## Repair and confirmation

Consolidate findings before repair. Describe the reader's problem and the understanding
wanted, not replacement wording. Repairs follow the repair notes in
[staycurrent-write](../staycurrent-write/SKILL.md). A fresh agent confirms each accepted
repair in context: it gets the finding and the changed passage, together with its
surroundings and later uses. This is normally an informed check. A repair that changes
what a figure teaches or how its lesson is explained needs an unprimed visual reader
for the affected figure or sequence before informed comparison. A bounded layout or
control repair uses rendered confirmation and retains the unaffected comprehension check.

Widen the check only for a concrete reason. A changed argument needs a reader check, a
changed claim needs a technical check, and changed media need rendered checks. Reuse
execution evidence while its code, data, environment and verifier still apply.

## Done

Respect the commissioned stopping point: a review-only assignment returns its findings
and evidence, without repairing or approving the article. For a complete article workflow,
review is done when every accepted critical or medium finding is repaired and confirmed,
the teach-back check passes, and visual comprehension and coverage have been established.
If an exit question is still not Supported after one
repair, the coordinator decides whether to repair again, rewrite the question or accept
the gap, and records why. Record the open low findings and any limits on the
evidence. Optional polish does not reopen review, and completing review does not
authorise publication.

Model reviewers can share the writer's blind spots and reward fluent prose. A passed
review records the evidence gathered; it does not by itself establish good writing.
