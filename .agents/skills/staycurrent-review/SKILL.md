# Review

Judge whether the reader ends up able to do what the commission promised, at the least
cost in time and effort. Reviewers report problems; they do not edit. The coordinator,
meaning whoever assigned the review, accepts findings, arranges repairs and decides when
the work is done.

## Lenses

A new or substantially rewritten article gets all three lenses, each from a separate,
fresh agent. When you are assigned one lens, load only that lens file.

- [Teach-back](lenses/teach-back.md): can a reader with only the article answer its exit
  questions?
- [Senior practitioner](lenses/practitioner.md): is it correct, complete at expert level,
  and better than the best alternative a reader could find?
- [Reader and visual](lenses/reader.md): does it read easily, does the prose stand alone,
  and do the figures follow the house style and work?

A **plan** gets the practitioner lens on its dossier, tree and exit questions: coverage,
ownership, depth and page count. A **collection** gets the practitioner lens and the
collection checks in the reader lens, across its reading routes. It reuses the article
reviews rather than repeating them. An article with no exit questions cannot be taught
back: report that to the coordinator, who has them written first.

## Inputs

Give each reviewer the audience, the commit or build being reviewed, and only the inputs
its lens lists. A first reader-lens review gets the finished article without plans,
author notes or earlier verdicts.

## Findings

For each finding, give its location, the reader's problem and the evidence: a passage, a
source or an observation. Report passing checks in a line.

- **Critical:** false or unsupported understanding, an incorrect action, an impossible
  argument, or a figure that contradicts the text.
- **Medium:** the reader must stall, reread or supply missing understanding, or cannot
  answer an exit question. Missing expert-level content belongs here too.
- **Low:** optional polish.

## Repair and confirmation

Consolidate findings before repair. Describe the reader's problem and the understanding
wanted, not replacement wording. Repairs follow the repair notes in
[staycurrent-write](../staycurrent-write/SKILL.md). A fresh agent confirms each accepted
repair in context: it gets the finding and the changed passage, together with its
surroundings and later uses. This is an informed check, not a new blind review.

Widen the check only for a concrete reason. A changed argument needs a reader check, a
changed claim needs a technical check, and changed media need rendered checks. Reuse
execution evidence while its code, data, environment and verifier still apply.

## Done

Review is done when every accepted critical or medium finding is repaired and confirmed,
and the teach-back check passes. If an exit question is still not Supported after one
repair, the coordinator decides whether to repair again, rewrite the question or accept
the gap, and records why. Record the open low findings and any limits on the
evidence. Optional polish does not reopen review, and completing review does not
authorise publication.
