---
name: staycurrent-continuity
description: Check explanatory continuity and shared example facts when drafting or repairing articles.
---

For a new draft or substantial rewrite, reread the complete piece from the intended reader's starting point, including figures, captions and controls. For a local repair, follow the changed explanation, its prerequisites and later uses; widen when those dependencies require it. Can the reader follow each new idea using the understanding developed so far? Follow the reference articles in how explanations, examples and figures establish meaning together.

First follow that reasoning with diagrams and animations omitted, including captions and controls. Example identities, necessary values, causal steps and results must remain understandable in prose. Then check that optional visuals use those facts consistently and reinforce the same explanation. Removing the visuals must not require repairing the prose.

Evolve familiar diagrams as the explanation develops, keeping roles, visual meanings and relationships recognisable. Show what starts the work and who performs it; omit context only when the causal path remains clear. Prefer familiar role labels and ordinary names where identification helps; use codes or implementation names when that distinction matters to the lesson.

For a collection, the shared example at `docs/coverage/<collection>-example.md` supplies the starting facts. Develop coherent worked scenarios from them and record scenario changes with the work. Agree shared extensions with the coordinator; a standalone writer updates the record when working in an existing collection. An independent article keeps its example facts with its own work, without inventing a collection. Keep qualifications that prevent a mistaken reader inference or action. Keep production bookkeeping in the work record.

After a repair, check its surrounding explanation and later uses. Keep a brief record of continuity decisions and unresolved dependencies with the work.
