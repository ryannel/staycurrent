description: Keep the reader's understanding and the collection's running example coherent through drafts and repairs.
---

Reread the complete piece from the intended reader's starting point, including figures, captions and controls. Can they follow each new idea using the understanding developed so far? Follow the reference articles in how explanations, examples and figures establish meaning together.

The shared example at `docs/coverage/<collection>-example.md` supplies the starting facts. Develop coherent worked scenarios from them and record scenario changes with the work. Agree shared extensions with the coordinator; a standalone writer updates the record. Keep qualifications that prevent a mistaken reader inference or action. Keep production bookkeeping in the work record.

After a repair, check its surrounding explanation and later uses. Keep a brief record of continuity decisions and unresolved dependencies with the work.
