## Writing in context

The opening is worth retaining. It identifies a shop, recognisable order fields, and a reason to read Country and Total. It does not need another hook or a wholesale change of voice. The experiment also repeats its setup locally and labels its counts as illustrative, which helps it stand on its own.

The difficulty lies in the missing explanatory steps. The house column-store example stays with the same fields and counts the pages each occupies. Ciechanowski's transmission passage similarly explains the contact that causes the next disc to move before drawing its conclusion. Here the prose jumps from an arrangement to a percentage, then changes subject to indexes. Restoring the physical reason for reading a page will do more than smoothing individual sentences. Evans's discussion of omitted system roles is relevant to that missing read unit; it is not a reason to expand this short piece into a database survey.

No optional voice rewrite is needed before these material repairs. Keep the shop and report, explain the two arrangements, derive their counts, and finish with the all-fields case.
