## Media choice

All instructional diagrams are original code-native SVG drawings. Exact arrows, repeated identities, stroke sequence and cross-view consistency are the subject, so editable vectors serve the task better than generated bitmap illustrations. They include internal light/dark palettes, direct labels and prose/alt equivalents. The shape styles are suggestions for human practice, not claims of a standard icon language. Timed practice is a suggested allocation, not measured performance.
