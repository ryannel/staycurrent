# Technical sources — first version

Checked 2026-10-25 using external primary documentation and the textbook authors' teaching material. No other Stay Current article body was read.

- Silberschatz, Korth and Sudarshan, Database System Concepts 7e, chapter 6 author-hosted slides: https://www.db-book.com/slides-dir/PDF-dir/ch6.pdf . Used for entity/relationship meaning, attributes on relationships, min/max cardinality and participation, ternary relationship distinctions, specialisation/subtype mapping.
- The same authors, chapter 7 author-hosted slides: https://www.db-book.com/slides-dir/PDF-dir/ch7.pdf . Used for dependencies as rules over legal instances, minimal candidate keys, 3NF/BCNF distinction, lossless joins versus dependency preservation, multivalued dependencies. Full formal tests are deliberately left to the planned child. The key and reconstruction examples are locally constructed from the shop facts.
- Microsoft, Database design basics: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/access/database-design-basics . Used for practical 1NF/2NF/3NF diagnosis. Its primary-key shorthand is qualified in this article: all candidate keys matter, including retained composite keys after adding a surrogate.
- Ronald Fagin (1977), Multivalued Dependencies and a New Normal Form for Relational Databases, IBM's research publication page: https://research.ibm.com/publications/multivalued-dependencies-and-a-new-normal-form-for-relational-databases . Confirmed 4NF is stronger than BCNF and concerns multivalued dependencies. The article uses a simple conceptual independence example, not a full formal test.
- PostgreSQL 18 Constraints: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-constraints.html . Confirmed composite keys, required referencing values, unique references, many-to-many and self-referential foreign keys; a parent reference does not require children or prevent cycles. No observed SQL execution is claimed.
- PostgreSQL 18 Designing JSON Documents: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/datatype-json.html#JSON-DOC-DESIGN . Confirmed complementary JSON/relational use, predictable document structure, and documents as units meaningful to business rules. Ordinary foreign keys require explicit referencing columns; JSON syntax validity does not automatically establish relationships inside arrays.

Research limit: a search found E. F. Codd's original Further Normalization PDF on a university host, but the fetch could not be read reliably; it is not cited as checked. No benchmark, migration or engine-specific observed result is presented. The focused local check runs only pure illustrative relational calculations, recorded under author-checks.md.
