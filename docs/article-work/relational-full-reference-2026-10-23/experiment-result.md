## Quality result

The original full draft did not meet the desired first-pass teaching standard. Its opening moved into four related tables before visibly establishing the reader’s first row/column mental model. Independent reviews also found a mixed example timeline, compressed application duties and caption jargon; the actual draft structure check found figure labels entering the heading outline. Practitioner and visual first reviews alone did not catch the foundation problem. Root accepted it after reading and inspecting the opening.

One fresh repair writer handled the five accepted decisions together. Four fresh confirmations found those repairs effective with no new findings. The final independent complete practitioner reading found no defects. The final blind complete editorial reading found no critical or medium defect and one optional low wording issue: “serialisation failure” lacks a local gloss, though the whole-decision retry instruction remains understandable. This optional polish is recorded, not repaired in another cycle. Root’s final reading found no additional substantial problem. See decisions.md and the unedited reports in reviews/.

## What the reference evidence supports

The initial writer did consult references and inspect selected rendered drawings before design. However, several large source-read outputs were truncated, and some further reference sections were read only after drafting. Complete reference delivery before design is therefore not established. The tool audit records requests/results, not private reasoning, and does not prove that truncation caused the figure choice.

For the repair, exact reference-content exports were delivered in ten bounded, untruncated sections before the first source write; rendered reference drawings were also inspected. Later final editorial calibration used clean exports with code markup decoded. This demonstrates more dependable input delivery for the repair, but that writer also had accepted review findings. Its success cannot establish uncoached repeatability.

The practical next experiment is to give a fresh writer the unchanged compact skills with reliably delivered complete references before design, then judge its untouched first return. This trial does not justify adding topic-specific skill rules or claiming the full-article process is reliable.
