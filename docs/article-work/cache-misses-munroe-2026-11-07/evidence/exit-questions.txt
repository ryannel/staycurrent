2. The shop runs 20 Server instances, each with per-process coalescing. Is that enough for P7's card (about 250 requests a second, 20 ms fill) and for the under-GBP-21.00 list? 3. Server A takes a 200 ms refresh lease for the list, then its query stalls for 900 ms. Trace what Server B does, how many source computations run and when, and which of A's later cache operations must be refused. What did the lease lifetime fail to bound, and what do you add at the source?

5. With XFetch and beta 1 on the list, roughly how long before expiry does the first refresh start, and about how many refreshes per expiry should you expect across the fleet? When is fixed jitter on refresh points enough instead, and why does neither help when Redis restarts empty?

6. Redis becomes unreachable at peak, with 20,000 card requests a second, a hit ratio near 94% and a source allowance of 2,000 fills a second. If every Server falls back to the database, how much source demand arrives, and how much does per-process coalescing remove? With two executing fills and a short deadline-checked queue per instance, what fraction of requests get a full card, and what should the rest receive?

Name the state the system is in, explain why it is not recovering on its own, and give the first two changes you would make.

8. Redis restarts empty at peak and admission holds fills at 2,000 a second. Under the example's popularity model, roughly how soon are the 1,000 most-requested cards back, and what share of requests do they serve? 9. A teammate proposes protecting the database with a 50-connection pool on each Server and no other limit. What does that allow across 20 instances, what do callers experience when fills slow from 20 ms to 200 ms, and what limits would you specify instead, including what must change when the fleet scales to 30 instances?

10. The card loader, its database client and the edge proxy each make up to three attempts. During a source brownout, how many source attempts can one request for one card cause, where should retries live, and how are they budgeted?
