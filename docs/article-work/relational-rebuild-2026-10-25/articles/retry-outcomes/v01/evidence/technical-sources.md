# Technical sources · checked 25 October 2026

Primary sources opened through the web tool:

- PostgreSQL18 Transaction Isolation: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/transaction-iso.html . Read Committed statement snapshots; conflicting guarded updates; ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING can decline an insert due to a concurrent row outside its statement snapshot. Grounds separate-statement recovery and the Repeatable Read/Serializable qualification.
- PostgreSQL18 INSERT: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-insert.html . Unique-index blocking, DO NOTHING and RETURNING only inserted/updated rows. Grounds gate SQL.
- PostgreSQL18 Serialization Failure Handling: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/mvcc-serialization-failure-handling.html .40001,40P01; whole transaction plus decision-logic retries; constraint errors may be persistent.
- PostgreSQL18 Cancelling queries in progress: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/libpq-cancel.html . Successful cancellation dispatch does not guarantee effect. No cancellation/disconnect inference of rollback.
- Malcolm Featonby, Amazon Builders’ Library: https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/ . Caller-scoped token, same token/different intent, atomic mutation/token record and retained history. Article’s shop protocol is its own concrete worked application, not a claim to implement an AWS service.
- Marc Brooker, Amazon Builders’ Library: https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/ . Redirected to https://builder.aws.com/content/3EumjoZascWd1oZiEgL8ORlv3qE/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter . Timeout does not exclude side effects; retry multiplication, bounds/backoff and jitter.
