Chapter 8 of Foundations of Databases timed out twice. Its material is not treated as read; chapter 11’s chase and the other author sources suffice. A CiteSeer copy of Fagin 1981 returned 403; the IBM-hosted storage copy above succeeded.

`verify.mjs` checks the model separately: all candidate keys, all 128 flattened-schema starting-set closures for cover equivalence, irredundancy of the reduced cover, projected FD preservation/non-preservation, 3NF/BCNF cases, successful and failed generic FD chases. It enumerates all 256 ternary relations over two values per domain, of which 166 obey the pairwise JD, and finds a counterexample among those for every non-trivial FD and MVD. This is a bounded model check, not a proof of universal absence from an arbitrary dependency theory. The article’s universal claims instead rely on the declared rules, mathematical argument and primary sources.

Ordinary join examples were independently run in an owned in-memory @electric-sql/pglite 0.5.8 instance. Actual `version()` was PostgreSQL 18.3 (PGlite 0.5.8), wasm32-unknown-emscripten, emcc 3.1.74, 32-bit. It confirmed the extra offer, four membership combinations, four lossy order reconstructions and the locally admitted adviser violation. The instance closed in finally. No native server, filesystem, real client concurrency, plan or performance claim is made. Full results are in `verification-results.json`.
