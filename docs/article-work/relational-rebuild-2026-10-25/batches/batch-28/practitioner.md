## Finding

**Medium — Figure 2 conflates a completed page write with persistence.** In `Checkpoint.astro`, the states displayed as **3 / 5** and **4 / 5** label page A and then page B “Required changes persisted”. At 3 / 5 the accompanying explanation says the checkpointer has written the products page and “must still complete the other required writes and synchronisation”; at 4 / 5 it says the earlier changes “are already stored”. I operated Next to these states on the rendered article. This gives the reader an earlier durability boundary than the operation shown establishes, undoing Figure 1's careful written/flushed distinction. PostgreSQL's [WAL configuration documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/wal-configuration.html) explicitly allows written pages to remain in the OS page cache until the end-of-checkpoint fsync. The [PG18 buffer manager](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/backend/storage/buffer/bufmgr.c) explains that a page flush initially passes bytes to the kernel; [CheckPointGuts](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/backend/access/transam/xlog.c) performs buffer writes before queued fsyncs. Keep the pre-completion states at the established write boundary and make the later synchronisation establish persistence. The old-redo/new-redo progression itself is correct.
