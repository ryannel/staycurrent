## Substantive finding

**Medium — replica agreement has no transferable teaching owner before its consumers.** Location: `collection-map.json`, `distribution`, `distributed-commit`, `placement`, `cluster-change` and the “Decide whether and how to distribute data” route. `distributed-commit` and `cluster-change` explicitly require “replica agreement”; `placement` requires it before deriving quorum survival. The generic `replicas` owner teaches copied-history progress and routing, while `distribution` teaches boundaries, skew, fan-out and coordination. Neither promises to establish how replicas agree on one authoritative history, what acknowledgements establish, or why a surviving collection of copies may be unable to make progress. The only explicit mechanism owner is `crdb-architecture` (“splits, leases and replica agreement”).

A reader following the five-page generic distribution route therefore reaches the distinction between replica agreement and transaction commit with one half assumed, and later cannot derive the quorum and membership arguments from the promised earlier understanding. A CockroachDB architecture detour would add an engine-specific page to a general decision route. Give the necessary foundational explanation an explicit generic owner before those consumers—`distribution` is a suitable parent-level home; `replicas` is another possible home if the route points there. This needs enough understanding to distinguish agreement within one replicated partition from an atomic decision across partitions; a separate full consensus lesson or extra article is not required. Keep CockroachDB’s native application with its current owner.
