## Sources checked on 26 October 2026

Primary versioned vendor documentation; these are documented contracts, not observations from our fixture.

- [Client connection parameters](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/connection-parameters): verify-full checks CA trust and address matching; validate the actual driver and URL. Do not substitute encryption alone for server identity.
- [TLS and PKI](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/security-reference/transport-layer-security) and [certificate creation](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/create-security-certificates-custom-ca): distinguish server/node/client certificates and protected private keys. Keep the worked deployment self-hosted; Cloud certificates and infrastructure are separate operational contracts.
- [Authorization](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/security-reference/authorization): ownership, public membership and broad node/root authority. It explicitly says column privileges are unsupported. The page's mention of no row-granularity grants must be read alongside native RLS, not as a claim that RLS is unavailable.
- [CREATE ROLE](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/create-role) and [GRANT](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/grant): login, membership, grant options and BYPASSRLS paths. Runtime should not own its tables, inherit admin or hold migration credentials.
- [SHOW GRANTS](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-grants): an object-only listing omits inherited grants; the principal-focused FOR form includes them. A native allow/deny test remains necessary.
- [RLS overview](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/row-level-security): applicable permissive/restrictive policy combination, default denial, USING/WITH CHECK, owner/admin exceptions and data-copy/constraint limitations. Session context remains the application's assertion. The documented example's default tenant is unsuitable for our missing-context requirement.
- [CREATE VIEW](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/create-view): underlying privilege checks use the view owner by default, or the caller with security_invoker. A support projection needs testing through that exact route; changing this option alone does not prove both column restriction and correct RLS enforcement.
- [SHOW RANGES](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-ranges): inspect range IDs, voters, other copies and leases with the required operating access. DETAILS has collection overhead. A location listing alone is not a health or capacity test.
- [Replication dashboard](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/ui-replication-dashboard): distinguish under-replicated and unavailable ranges; non-voters are excluded from the displayed under-replication value. Use a relevant time window and metric scope.
- [cockroach node](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/cockroach-node): status timestamps can be stale; --ranges and --decommission expose different evidence. Per-node range counts can refer to the same ranges, so their sum is not a distinct-range count.
- [Node shutdown](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/node-shutdown): drain versus permanent removal, pre-flight destination checks, progress and final membership, and recommissioning's state boundary. Pre-flight excludes remaining disk space. Submit the complete approved removal set together when intentionally removing several nodes; this plan demonstrates one node. Do not confuse ending the waiting CLI with cancelling membership change. A completed decommission is permanent.
