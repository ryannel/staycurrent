## Primary sources and consequential checks

Checked 26 October 2026 against versioned CockroachDB v26.2 documentation. These are documentation-based planning conclusions, not native observations.

- [Transactions](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/transactions#isolation-levels) and [Serializable transactions](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/demo-serializable): two native isolation levels, Serializable default and serial-order guarantee. Do not reproduce a PostgreSQL four-level menu or treat serializability as automatically repairing an incorrect business protocol.
- [Read Committed](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/read-committed#read-committed-transaction-behavior): statement snapshots, cross-statement anomalies and internal statement retries. v26.2 explicitly provides stronger within-statement isolation than PostgreSQL Read Committed. Avoid “never retries/never returns 40001”: deadlocks and rare failures after results begin streaming remain exceptions. Check `SHOW transaction_isolation` within an attempt; the session default alone is insufficient, and disabled Read Committed support upgrades requests.
- [Transaction layer: read snapshots and closed timestamps](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/architecture/transaction-layer#closed-timestamps): the safety explanation needs closed-time evidence plus application through the corresponding Raft position. A largest timestamp on disk or a node's wall clock is insufficient. The accepted architecture already introduces MVCC versions and current serving authority; general HLC/uncertainty analysis stays with distributed snapshots.
- [AS OF SYSTEM TIME](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/as-of-system-time) and [SET TRANSACTION](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/set-transaction): database history, read-only scope, timestamp/function forms and transaction-wide selection before data work. No locking or write authority comes from the historical view. `with_min_timestamp` requires a trustworthy bound with the documented timestamp type; a pre-commit application clock reading is not supplied as such a bound. Do not invent a native causal-token acquisition recipe.
- [Follower reads](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/follower-reads): exact and bounded modes, strong GLOBAL reads, convenience timestamp, nearest-replica routing and limitations. Bounded mode requires an implicit single-statement transaction, one row and no index join. A covering primary-key P7 read fits the intended example. `nearest_only` can fail rather than allow another serving path. Exact mode can reach the leaseholder or wait on an intent. Do not promise the same snapshot across separately evaluated bounds. Strong-read exceptions include long-running reads whose timestamp falls behind closed time; ordinary current-read routing must therefore be qualified rather than stated as an absolute.
- [Storage layer: time travel, garbage collection and protected timestamps](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/architecture/storage-layer#garbage-collection), [replication controls](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/configure-replication-zones#replication-zone-variables) and [SHOW ZONE CONFIGURATIONS](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-zone-configurations): effective retention must be inspected, not inferred from copied default examples. Explain GC's history boundary and storage trade, without promising indefinite survival for a retained timestamp or prescribing manual protected-timestamp management. Historical schema handling exists, but schema-change/recovery procedures belong elsewhere.
- [EXPLAIN ANALYZE](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/explain-analyze#statement-plan-tree-properties): executed evidence includes historical timestamp, isolation, `used follower read`, SQL nodes and KV nodes.
