## Primary sources checked, 26 October 2026

- [Multi-region overview, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/multiregion-overview): cluster versus database regions, node `region`/`zone` localities, primary region and separate locality/survival choices. A home region is not an exclusive storage guarantee. Super regions and restricted placement need distinct treatment if actual domiciling requirements arise; no compliance claim is commissioned here.
- [Table localities, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/table-localities): regional/table, regional/row and global choices; regional-by-row index partitioning and conditional local-first search. “Optimised” does not establish that every statement or transaction is local. Secondary regions are not a supported combination with regional-by-row tables; omit that optional feature from the worked setup.
- [ALTER TABLE, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/alter-table#set-locality): native locality syntax, hidden region column, explicit assignment/update and gateway fallback. Its regional-index examples distinguish implicit global uniqueness from explicitly region-scoped uniqueness. Auto-rehoming is off by default; foreign-key home inference is a separately enabled preview. Preserve defaults and avoid teaching either optional mechanism as automatic. Full SQL and expected output still require the pinned runtime.
- [Global tables, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/global-tables): up-to-date reads, read-mostly fit, write commit-wait and survival controlled independently at database level. Its troubleshooting section admits sporadic slow reads; avoid “every read is always local” as an unconditional latency guarantee. Deep protocol/clock-setting tuning is outside this article.
- [Survival goals, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/multiregion-survival-goals): database-wide zone default, region goal, minimum three database regions and five-voter two–two–one case. Regional writes need remote agreement. Explain a path to progress after a failure, not zero interruption or a measured recovery deadline.
- [ALTER DATABASE, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/alter-database): region/survival statements and the distinction between `num_voters` and `num_replicas`; native read-only survival-goal and zone-configuration inspection. Prefer the high-level multi-region policies over a bespoke zone-configuration tutorial.
- [SHOW CREATE, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-create) and [SHOW REGIONS, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-regions): declared database regions/survival and table locality versus cluster inventory.
- [SHOW RANGES, v26.2](https://docs.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v26.2/show-ranges): voting/non-voting replicas and replica localities; `WITH DETAILS` adds leaseholder/locality and incurs cross-node work. This is point-in-time observation, not proof all future placements or queries will follow the same path.
