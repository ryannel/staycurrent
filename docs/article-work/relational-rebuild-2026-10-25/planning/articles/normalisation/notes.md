## Technical checks and primary sources

- [Silberschatz, Korth and Sudarshan, Database System Concepts, chapter 7](https://www.db-book.com/slides-dir/PDF-dir/ch7.pdf): slides 12–17, 19–28, 34–55 and 60–63 verify keys, FD-relative binary losslessness, closure, projected dependencies, exact 3NF/BCNF definitions, minimal-cover work and synthesis with a key component. The binary intersection criterion is necessary only under FD-only assumptions. The dependency-preservation check concerns all implied component dependencies, not merely original written ones.
- [Abiteboul, Hull and Vianu, Foundations of Databases, chapter 11](https://webdam.di.ens.fr/Alice/pdfs/Chapter-11.pdf), especially §§11.2–11.3: lossless reconstruction, the chase example, local preservation, BCNF decomposition, synthesis and the different information capacity of independently maintained component tables. Chapter 8 fetch failed; no claim relies on having read it.
- [Bernstein, Synthesizing Third Normal Form Relations from Functional Dependencies (1976)](https://apollo.inf.upol.cz/~urbanec/teaching/2021/data2/files/bernstein.pdf), §§2–6: original synthesis research, minimality/prime terminology and dependency inference. Use the modern textbook algorithm rather than presenting this paper’s specialised synthesis construction as an identical procedure.
- [Fagin, Multivalued Dependencies and a New Normal Form for Relational Databases (1977)](https://s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/res-files/500-tods77.pdf), §§2–3: tuple swapping, complementary sets, binary reconstruction and 4NF. Several values alone do not imply independence.
- [Fagin, Normal Forms and Relational Database Operators (1979)](https://s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/res-files/500-sigmod79.pdf), §§1–2: JDs, decompositions requiring more than binary stages and PJ/NF. Say implied by the **set** of key dependencies; requiring one candidate key in every component is an overstrong definition.
- [Fagin, A Normal Form for Relational Databases That Is Based on Domains and Keys (1981)](https://s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/res-files/500-tods81.pdf): DK/NF has a different constraint-based aim; avoid promising a general practical transformation or using the paper’s specialised anomaly theorem as a claim that all business mistakes disappear.
- [Darwen, Temporal Data and the Relational Model](https://www.dcs.warwick.ac.uk/~hugh/TTM/TemporalData.Warwick.pdf), slide 29: 6NF is irreducibility; temporal formulations revise the projection/join operators. Keep this distinction brief here and do not invent temporal procedures for the history article.
