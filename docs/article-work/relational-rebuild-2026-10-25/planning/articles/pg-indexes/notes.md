## Primary sources checked

Checked PostgreSQL 18 documentation on 25 October 2026. Use versioned links and record the actual runtime version if native evidence is added.

- [Operator Classes and Operator Families](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-opclass.html): classes bind comparison behaviour; families include cross-type operators. Pattern classes support prefix-pattern searches under the documented collation conditions, but do not replace ordinary locale range ordering.
- [Index Types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-types.html) and [Indexes and ORDER BY](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-ordering.html): access-method selection and operator support; distinguish B-tree sorted value output from GiST/SP-GiST class-specific distance ordering.
- [Hash Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/hash-index.html): stored four-byte hashes, equality rechecks, single key column, no uniqueness, overflow and foreground growth. Modern hash indexes are crash recoverable; do not repeat obsolete warnings that they are unlogged.
- [GIN Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/gin.html): extracted keys and postings; operator-specific certainty; pending-list work must still be searched and eventually merged. A large pending list or foreground cleanup can hurt latency. This is a cost explanation, not an autovacuum tuning procedure.
- [GiST Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/gist.html) and [SP-GiST Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/spgist.html): distinct extensible frameworks, built-in range/point classes, class-specific ordering and exactness. Do not label every GiST leaf match approximate, or every SP-GiST method a quadtree.
- [BRIN Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/brin.html): physical page-range summaries, lossy heap checking, pages_per_range, summarisation and unsummarised ranges. Min/max is the chosen example class, not a definition covering every BRIN class. Autosummarise is not enabled by default; exact commands and maintenance scheduling belong elsewhere.
- [Index-Only Scans and Covering Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-index-only-scans.html), [Multicolumn Indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-multicolumn.html) and [CREATE INDEX](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-createindex.html): do not transfer B-tree's multicolumn or covering rules indiscriminately to the other methods. GIN cannot reconstruct the complete indexed value for an index-only scan; GiST/SP-GiST support depends on the class. Retain the parent's separate visibility requirement.
- [Using EXPLAIN](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/using-explain.html): index recheck rejection, filters, buffers and the fact that ANALYZE executes the statement. [PostgreSQL 18 bitmap representation source](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/backend/nodes/tidbitmap.c) and [bitmap heap executor](https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/backend/executor/nodeBitmapHeapscan.c) confirm that exact tuple offsets and the need for predicate recheck are independent. A printed Recheck Cond alone does not show that any row failed it.
