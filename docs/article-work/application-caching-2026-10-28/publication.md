# Application caching publication

29 October 2026. The user authorised publication of this collection and a separate
visual review of the relational collection. The nine caching articles now use
`/learn/databases/application-caching/`; Redis remains the worked implementation
among possible caches.

## Scope

- Public article routes, publication metadata, breadcrumbs and collection navigation.
- All nine reading-directory and search entries, plus a link from the database guide.
- Public downloads and updated article links. Review and style-study routes remain
  excluded from normal builds.
- Three substantive visual repairs from the [economy review](visual-economy-review/review.md):
  a direct working-set comparison, separated replay labels, and visible source-admission
  deadlines. [Repair details](visual-economy-review/repairs.md) record continuity and focused checks.

The coordinator accepted those three repairs after inspecting their actual rendered
initial and selected intermediate/final states in article context, at desktop and
375 px, including light and dark presentations. The slow-source frame shows work
continuing beyond the caller deadline. The source-speed controls select their
correct variants. Hidden playback limits were addressed with labelled snapshots
from the real renderers; this does not claim a fresh pacing certification of every
animation. The existing shared player and unaffected checks are retained. Four
optional simplifications remain; they do not block this publication.
