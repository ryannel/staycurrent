# Application caching collection

Commissioned 28 October 2026: write the application caching collection using the
restored Stay Current skills and dense-prose check. Deliver reviewed drafts;
publication has not been authorised.

Take an intelligent engineer from newcomer to expert practitioner in application
caching, optimising useful understanding against reader time and effort. Use Redis
for concrete implementation and practice, presenting it as one of many choices.
Teach general principles and identify implementation-specific behaviour and
guarantees. Introduce alternatives where they change a practical decision.

The scope is source-backed application data and reusable results: deciding whether
and where to cache, identifying an answer, keeping it acceptably fresh, handling
misses and failure, managing capacity, and implementing and operating the design.
Redis's other roles can be named at the boundary; they do not expand this into a
general Redis course. Derive article boundaries from the teaching rather than a
target article count. Earlier caching plans and the removed experiment are context,
not the scope or prose template for this commission.

Use the three fixed reference articles for writing and visual teaching. Plan the
recursive tree, arrange one independent tree review, write with fresh workers, and
review complete articles for technical accuracy, teaching/writing and rendered
behaviour. Resolve accepted substantive findings and perform one assembled-route
check, retaining unaffected evidence. Keep the source-backed shop example coherent.

The coordinator owns preview/build integration. Drafts live in
`src/articles/application-caching/` and render only with
`APPLICATION_CACHING_DRAFT_PREVIEW=1` at `/review/application-caching/`.
Normal publication, navigation and search stay gated on later authorisation.
