The body then qualifies the models and supplies implementation detail. Invented timings, proportional measures, execution evidence and hypothetical traces are distinguished in captions.

The Sequence diagrams, Indexes, and Storage and durability reference captures in `/private/tmp/application-caching-references/` calibrated this judgement.
