description: Write or substantially revise an article by imitating the three fixed reference articles in its writing and teaching.
---

Study [Sequence diagrams](../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) as complete finished articles. Follow their drawings through the explanations they develop before designing your own. Write an article that could sit beside them and feel written by the same author, adapting their teaching to what this reader already understands.

Use the plan for coverage and relationships, in the context of parent, sibling and child articles. Reshape its headings, sequence, examples and depth wherever needed to meet the references' standard. Summarise and link relevant finished children. Follow the collection's [example record and continuity](../staycurrent-continuity/SKILL.md); propose extensions centrally.

Use [staycurrent-figures](../staycurrent-figures/SKILL.md) for figures, tables, code and interactive tools. Coordinate rendered checks with the designated preview/build owner.

Check consequential claims against primary sources; list them under Sources and further reading. Let the fixed references lead the teaching. Distinguish illustrative models from observed results; keep applicable execution evidence with the work.

Reread the finished piece against the references, including captions and controls, and run the continuity pass. Return it for the commissioned review; standalone complete articles arrange [article review](../staycurrent-review/SKILL.md).
