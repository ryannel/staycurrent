Reference headings give economical names to developed concepts; the draft often turns an outcome into a proposition. Reviews judged understandable, accurate section labels, not likeness to the reference author. Root also noticed formal headings and wrongly treated them as optional preference. Narrow prior opening successes do not establish the same quality under a full survey brief.

## Astra process diagnosis

Initial reference delivery demonstrably clipped at the outer tool-output boundary; bounded delivery was introduced too late for that writer. Complete repair/final-reference access did not fix weak calibration: the unchanged headings still passed. Ten reviews followed the current prescribed repair schedule; additional same-threshold reviews are not a solution. Isolate a fresh writer from prior drafts/feedback, deliver and verify complete bounded reference text before design, simplify the commission’s parent-versus-child distinction, and show the untouched first version before external review or repair. Hashes establish identity and observed outputs establish delivery; neither proves understanding.
