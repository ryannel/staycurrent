# Relational databases: fresh planning commission

**Topic:** Relational databases.

**Brief:** Take an intelligent engineer who is new to relational databases to
expert practice in using them: choosing a suitable system, designing and
querying data, building correct applications, diagnosing behaviour, and
operating and recovering the service. Teach mechanisms and implementation
differences where they enable sound relational engineering decisions.

This expertise target belongs to relational databases throughout the tree. Each
article contributes only the depth needed for that original goal; it does not
reset the target to specialist expertise in its own topic. Judge additional
detail by the decision, explanation, diagnosis or procedure it enables for a
relational practitioner, against the reader's time and effort.

Build and review the complete article/heading tree, then stop for discussion
before writing articles. Use the updated skills and orchestrator-managed batch
reviews. There is no preset article count, branch list or vendor curriculum.

Start from this commission with fresh builders. Existing relational articles,
previous coverage maps and the stopped planning run are not structural inputs.
The three fixed examples are the teaching/style references for that review
perspective. Relational sits within Databases and storage; neighbouring families
and the separate shared distributed-systems programme are outside this commission,
except for context needed to understand relational decisions.

Canonical proposal: `docs/coverage/relational-fanout-2026-10-19.yaml`.
Assignments, review findings and repairs stay beside this commission. This
planning run does not change publication status or public navigation.
