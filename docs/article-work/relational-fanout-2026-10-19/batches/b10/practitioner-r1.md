# Practitioner review — b10, round 1

Reviewed a32–a34 and their supplied contexts against the original relational-practice commission, with conditional candidate acceptance as the depth limit. No other review report was read. Verdicts assess planned checks and decisions, not product suitability or unexecuted acceptance evidence.

## a32 — Checking an embedded choice with SQLite

**Pass. No required repair.** File ownership, actual loaded library, writer overlap, connection initialisation and independent recovery copies are necessary adoption checks that generic server-oriented examples would not supply. The journal/busy-handling depth is justified by whether the intended deployment can satisfy its contention and acknowledged-write requirements. Process termination is explicitly distinguished from power-loss evidence. The final accept/reject decision, limits and reassessment triggers keep this from becoming a SQLite curriculum. No further embedded-engine branch is needed.

A minor future-link clarification: “common durability article” should resolve to the actual transaction durability explanation and operational recovery owner, rather than imply a new standalone article. This is not a coverage repair.

## a33 — Checking a server choice with PostgreSQL

**Pass. No required repair.** This is appropriately a compact acceptance adapter to the shared relational methods. It verifies the concrete driver, service tier, pool mode, privileges and configuration without reteaching the PostgreSQL mechanisms already used in the corpus. The starting-configuration record ties settings to reasons and evidence, and required workload, recovery and upgrade checks determine acceptance. Neither product-name familiarity nor a managed-service promise can substitute for those gates. No additional PostgreSQL specialist branch is justified by the commission.

## a34 — Checking a distributed choice with CockroachDB

**Pass. No required repair.** Distribution must first satisfy a requirement the simpler deployment fails. Placement and coordination detail is bounded by predicting latency, residency and named-failure results, rather than teaching consensus independently. Compatibility tests explicitly challenge PostgreSQL protocol assumptions; retry ownership and ambiguous outcomes reuse the common application contract. Production-topology evidence, degraded capacity, restoration after logical loss and total resource/network cost are necessary adoption judgements. Untestable cases remain named gaps, so an illustrative cluster cannot imply production acceptance. The plan can terminate here without a CockroachDB or distributed-systems curriculum.

## Batch verdict

All three plans pass. Their implementation detail is conditional on adopting a candidate and changes a concrete acceptance decision. No required repair or additional article is identified.
