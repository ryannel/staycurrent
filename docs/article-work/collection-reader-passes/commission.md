# Relational collection: four reader passes

Requested 15 October 2026. Baseline: `afbf6f7`, served from a frozen build on
localhost:4324 while repairs use the ordinary preview. The user requested four
sentence-by-sentence perspectives, inspection of every illustration in context,
implementation of improvements, updates to the writing and review skills, and a
commit and push to origin main.

Read the overview, then all eight available articles in the revised tree:
Designing your data; Constraints in depth; Tables and JSON; Querying your data;
Pagination and large results; Transactions and concurrency; Isolation and
snapshots; Handling conflicts and uncertain outcomes. Planned outlines are not
finished articles. Practice and sandbox destinations are followed where the
explanation sends a reader there.

The perspectives are blind sequential comprehension, adversarial interpretation,
application practice, and literary/structural editing. Each receives a fresh
conversation without earlier article discussions or other reviewers' findings.
The blind reader receives only the artifact, audience and context-free reading
guidance. The other roles remain independent but may verify sources or inspect
implementation after reading a relevant rendered passage.

Each reader records questions at the point they arise, includes headings, code,
results and labels, and examines actual rendered illustrations and interactive
states. They preserve resolved questions as well as remaining gaps. These are
agent observations, not evidence collected from human novice participants.

The coordinator will distinguish necessary repairs from optional curiosity and
preferences, preserve useful disagreement, and recheck changed explanations and
behaviour. Skill revisions should capture demonstrated editorial decisions rather
than make this four-reader assignment or this collection's examples mandatory for
every future piece.
