Third normal form when candidate keys overlap

The formal definition also handles tables with overlapping candidate keys. For a dependency that determines a column outside the determining set, either the determining columns must identify a row, or that dependent column must belong to some candidate key. Our example has no such overlapping keys, so the simpler reasoning above is sufficient. The further reading develops those cases and the stricter Boyce–Codd normal form.
