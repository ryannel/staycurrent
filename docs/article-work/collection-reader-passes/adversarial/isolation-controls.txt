What this model shows

This fixed schedule models PostgreSQL 18 ordinary reads. It is not connected to a database and does not time execution. Repeatable Read establishes its snapshot at the first query here, not at BEGIN. The model excludes the report’s own writes, locking reads, conflicting writes, failures and replicas. EUR 22 is a new catalogue offer; O12’s recorded EUR 18 purchase price is unrelated and unchanged.
