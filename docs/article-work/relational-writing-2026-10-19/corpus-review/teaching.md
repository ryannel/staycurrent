## Optional improvement

**T-OPT-01 — a23, “Claim work without keeping a transaction open during the call”, Figure 2, “A lease owns a database transition”.** The three variants correctly change worker/database states, and the slow-worker endpoint correctly rejects A’s database completion while leaving external duplication unresolved. However, the figure conveys the critical overlap almost entirely through changing text cards and a five-item step strip. A newcomer must mentally place A’s continuing external call across the lease expiry and B’s new claim. A small two-worker timeline would make that cause easier to see and recall, closer to the reference sequence article’s visible waiting/ownership intervals. Smallest adequate refinement: retain the existing variants, cards and controls; add A/B activity lanes, a lease-expiry marker and the attempt identity at claim/completion. In the slow variant, keep A’s activity visibly continuing past expiry while B owns attempt 2; mark A’s late database completion as rejected. This is optional because the current explanation and comparison are unambiguous and sufficient for the practical decision. It does not require a new article or protocol.
