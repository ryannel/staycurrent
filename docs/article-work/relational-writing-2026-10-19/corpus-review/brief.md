# Review the written relational corpus

Apply `staycurrent-review` in corpus mode for your assigned perspective directly, without another team. The goal is to take an intelligent newcomer to expert practice in relational databases, optimising useful understanding against reader time and effort. A descendant contributes to that goal; it does not promise expert specialism in its own subject.

Read the complete canonical tree and every finished article. The orchestrator supplies the complete source/reading manifest and frozen preview when all articles are ready. Readable copies preserve prose, code, tables and contextual links; their figure cues do not replace the actual interactive articles or components. Source articles are authoritative. Keep your own per-article coverage notes so a long review does not lose earlier context.

Assess the actual finished collection independently. Do not use the other corpus reviewers' reports as evidence. Existing individual engine, model, keyboard, phone and structural checks can be retained; repeat a check when a new cross-article concern requires it.

The three assigned perspectives are:

- **Completeness and depth:** Does the whole collection supply the understanding, decisions, failure reasoning and practical work needed for the original relational goal? Find consequential gaps, contradictory contracts, missing prerequisites and uneven depth. Judge the complete reading route rather than asking each leaf to become a specialist curriculum.
- **Structure, overlap and adversarial consolidation:** Challenge the reading and navigation cost of every article and section. Look for repeated explanations, competing ownership, unnecessary detours and details that belong lower down. Propose concrete cuts or merges with what useful understanding would be lost. Seek the smallest coherent corpus that meets the goal, without a page quota.
- **Teaching and flow:** Read all three fixed examples in full, including their figures, and assess the writing and teaching against them. Follow the actual reader journeys. Does a parent teach a useful first case and introduce deeper questions in context? Do children build on that foundation? Do interactive figures carry important mechanisms throughout? Assess the actual watching and reading rhythm and the usefulness of controls, not merely their presence. Check continuity between examples and the transition from explanation to practical judgement.

Return concrete findings with article IDs, section or figure locations, the reader consequence and the smallest adequate repair. Separate required changes from optional improvements and state what you read. Do not replan an unchanged approved curriculum without evidence from the finished work. Save your report in this directory. The orchestrator coordinates repairs and sends affected findings back for recheck.
