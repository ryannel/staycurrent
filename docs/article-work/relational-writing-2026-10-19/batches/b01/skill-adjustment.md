and a paging result display that did not expose the saved boundary or skipped

important explanations throughout, letting readers watch causes change results
and inspect steps, with readable pacing and usable phone comparisons. Added one
sentence to teaching review: judge visual teaching across the article, including
mechanisms left to prose or code, playback, keyboard use and phone comparisons. No numerical
figure target, new curriculum or prose template was added. Both skills pass the
skill-creator validator; their .claude mirrors match. In-progress a03 was notified
to reread the update before returning its draft.

actually improves the articles; the wording alone does not establish that.
