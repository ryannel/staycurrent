## Teaching thread and scope

The parent already teaches first anomalies and current/agreed price meaning.
This article starts there and extends its actual class-booking contract with
optional services. It identifies both mixed-relation candidate keys under the
parent’s one retained customer–session booking assumption. Partial and transitive
dependencies lead to service/category owners. An adviser assignment example
develops overlapping candidate keys, BCNF and the separate lossless/local-rule
questions, then chooses a constrained repeated service attribute for enforcement.
Venue languages/facilities expose independent versus correlated sets and empty
membership. Optional-service subtotals supply the redundancy and repair contract.

Five figures let readers change storage, a change or the requirement and inspect
actual row/result consequences. Comparison retains the current step. Playback
waits at least twelve seconds, budgeting all visible stage/panel/result text,
selected comparison labels and narration at 320 ms per word, plus five seconds
to compare. Back/Next endpoints remain focusable with aria-disabled
and bounded actions. All playback is explicit; there is no automatic motion on
load. Backgrounding the page pauses playback. On narrow screens cards stack,
full select labels remain available and controls wrap. Figure titles are
figcaptions, with no internal heading tags. Transcript disclosures follow the
selected comparison; server rendering supplies a readable initial state.
