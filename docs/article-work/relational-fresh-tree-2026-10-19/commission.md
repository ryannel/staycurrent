# Relational: fresh planning commission

Build and review the complete article and heading tree for relational databases
from scratch, using the current orchestration, breakdown and review skills.
Stop before writing articles so the user can review the plan.

## Topic and brief

**Topic:** Relational databases.

**Brief:** Take an intelligent engineer who is new to relational databases to
expert practice: choosing an appropriate relational system, designing and
building applications with it, diagnosing behaviour, and operating and recovering
it with sound judgement. Teach the mechanisms and implementation differences
needed to make those decisions. Optimise useful understanding against reader
time, effort and navigation; seek a coherent corpus rather than a product
catalogue. Let the breakdown and reviews determine the necessary depth and
article boundaries.

Relational sits within Databases and storage. Other database families are
neighbouring subjects: compare or link where needed, without planning their
collections here. The separate shared distributed-systems programme remains
parked; teach relational mechanisms needed for this commission in context.

## Planning boundaries

Do not use the existing relational articles, coverage maps or previous page trees
as a starting structure. The three fixed reference articles remain the writing
and teaching examples for reviewers who need them. There is no inherited page
count, vendor branch or required list of article titles.

Each worker plans only its assigned article and child stubs, owns its two
independent reviews and repairs, and returns a YAML breakdown. The orchestrator
expands children separately, then reconciles the three whole-tree reviews.

The canonical proposal is `docs/coverage/relational-2026-10-19.yaml`.
Work and review records stay in this directory. Planning does not change public
reading, navigation or publication status.
