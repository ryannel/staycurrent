## Conflicts and choices

- Fig 3: both reviewers asked for a replay of measured data rather than a model, and I chose replay. The laptop was heavily loaded (load average 7 to 38), so per-option runs are noisy. I chose by smallest longest call, not fastest wall time. Fastest wall time had picked a COUNT 10 run with a 7 ms OS-preempted call, which would have shown a spike SCAN did not cause.
- The new walk times are faster than the old draft's (COUNT 10 9.9 s vs 11 s; KEYS 86 ms vs about 100). This is because the client now reads replies in bulk. All prose numbers for Fig 3 now come from `walks.mjs`. Figure 2 still uses the earlier 99.4 ms KEYS measurement, which the caption states.
- Fig 6 data stays Mac-only (I have no Linux host). Linux behaviour is given from source, and the caption says so.
