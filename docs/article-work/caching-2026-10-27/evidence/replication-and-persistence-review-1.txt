## S1. Source: src/aof.c flushAppendOnlyFile (8.10.3), everysec postpones write(2) of aof_buf up to 2000 ms while a background fsync runs; src/server.c beforeSleep sends replies (handleClientsWithPendingWrites) whether or not the postponed buffer was written (the ordering comment names only appendfsync=always). So a process crash during a postponement loses acknowledged writes still in Redis's own buffer.
## S2. Source: src/server.c writeCommandsDeniedByDiskError: stop_writes_on_bgsave_err && saveparamslen > 0 && lastbgsave_status == C_ERR -> MISCONF (RDB); separately, any AOF write error (aof_last_write_status == C_ERR) refuses writes regardless of stop-writes-on-bgsave-error. Confirmed by E3 (save '' -> no MISCONF) and E3b (default save points -> MISCONF).
## S3. Source: redis.conf 8.10.3 'disable-thp yes' and server.c THPDisable(): prctl(PR_SET_THP_DISABLE) when the kernel THP setting is 'always'.
