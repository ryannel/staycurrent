# Access and persistence: production brief

Commissioned 15 October 2026: batch 1 of the relational completion plan. Produce
Indexes, How a query runs, and Storage and durability as reviewed working drafts.
The collection's existing article boundaries remain in force.

Readers know tables, simple SQL and the purpose of transactions. Each article must
orient a direct entrant and explain the mechanisms needed for its own decisions.
The query-execution article extends Querying your data with alternative strategies
and estimates; it must offer a distinct investigation after the parent's concrete
plan. Indexes connects ordered structure to choosing and validating an access path.
Storage connects physical changes to the durability promise and recovery boundary.

Use identified PostgreSQL 18 examples, with versioned primary evidence. Keep
illustrative models, executed SQL and documented failure behaviour distinguishable.
Each article owns its brief, evidence and verification material in its named work
folder. No benchmark conclusion follows from a small fixture or a browser runtime.

Authors work in separate page/component directories. The coordinator integrates
collection status and navigation, verifies the site, and commissions independent
reviews once the assembled baseline exists. A fresh reader receives only assembled
reader artifacts, audience and context-free reading/style guidance. That reader
records questions in sequence across the three pages before receiving intentions.
Separate technical and editorial reviews challenge correctness, practical outcomes,
parent/child boundaries and explanatory progression. Preserve their baseline
findings, resolve material issues and recheck the changes.

Inspect diagrams and interactions at desktop and phone widths in both themes,
exercise consequential control states and keyboard access, execute relevant SQL,
and run `pnpm build`. Record actual scope and limitations in the final review note.
Completion means ready as working drafts; publishing is a separate decision.
