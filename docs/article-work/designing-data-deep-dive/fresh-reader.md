## What the article taught

The design follows record meaning and dependencies. It distinguishes repeated references from copied current facts, and historical prices from current offers. Keys encode permitted identities, types encode representations, and constraints turn some of the schema's assumptions into enforced rules. Rows, columns and JSON are judged by identity, validation and patterns of change. I did not need to supply missing database theory to follow the central argument. I supplied SQL alias/join syntax at the first mug query; that is the clearest local teaching gap.
