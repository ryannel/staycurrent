## Diagnosis

Trial 2 required four fresh full reviews to find nothing required on pages twice as long. Proposed changes, pending the user's decision: fresh reviewers recheck accepted findings and the repaired sections in context; one fresh full read at the end, looping only on correctness; required means wrong, misleading or blocking; practitioner and teaching each round, editorial and visual once before the final read; a length budget from the breakdown with value checked inside teaching; smallest-change repairs; verification scripts that do not grow with each round.
