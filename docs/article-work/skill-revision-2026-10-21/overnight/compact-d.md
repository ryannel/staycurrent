## Exact policy changes

Relative to Compact A + D quality:

- Replace rising repair floors with stable acceptance: accepted critical/medium defects require repair whenever found; low polish is optional. Decisions identify the concrete reader effect against starting knowledge and the piece's promise, preserving reasons and evidence. Coherence, prerequisites, patch-like passages and unusable media remain substantive even when technically true. No round, floor, time or cap waives a required repair/check.
- Require all four independent article perspectives, with their complete prescribed checks and distinct inputs, on the same frozen first version. After repair, use fresh informed relevant-perspective confirmations of problems, pass conditions, surroundings and dependencies, widening wherever prior judgement is invalidated. Added/restructured material receives teaching placement checking; overall argument/order changes require complete teaching/editorial assessment. Changed claims/examples and media/components require corresponding expert/media coverage across affected material/articles, including current rendered control use and appropriate primary-source/native verification.
- After substantive repair and specialist confirmation, require one fresh blind complete rendered reading using editorial inputs, without plans, findings, decisions, repair history or writer notes. It assesses editorial checks, coherence, prerequisite order, examples, teaching, practical judgement and new defects. It cannot replace invalidated specialist checks. Later local repairs get fresh confirmation; invalidate the whole-article judgement and a fresh complete reading repeats. Optional edits do not restart review, but any invalidated evidence/judgement still needs checking.
- Remove competing automatic article rounds 2/3 and blanket final editorial/visual repeats. Breakdown/tree/corpus retain every listed perspective and full checks each round, with round 3 the last full round, followed by fresh substantive repair confirmations until pass, including new defects; these modes have no separate final reader. Orchestrator routing follows the article schedule for corpus-induced article repairs and the corpus schedule for corpus findings. Required unfinished work means incomplete review, never publication authority.
- Revision handoffs name changes beyond findings plus affected dependencies/argument and return applicable verification. The author's complete reread and full continuity audit remain mandatory and distinct from specialist confirmation and the independent final reading.

B/C semantics are integrated rather than copied as an additional manual. No new roles, checklists or perspective checks are added. Every assignment remains fresh; first/full reviewers and final readers remain unexposed to earlier versions/reports, while repair confirmations explicitly receive problems and pass conditions.

D quality's connected-explanation objective, plain prose, necessary qualifications, domain-general example record, compact continuity decisions and phone legibility criteria are preserved. Relational instructions remain conditional. Mechanical receipts retain only relevant mechanical records/provenance and exclude author teaching notes, prior quality verdicts and review findings; mixed records require extraction. Eleven inherited files outside the three integrated policy files are byte-identical to the effective Compact A + D quality input, including all five perspective files and both technical references.
