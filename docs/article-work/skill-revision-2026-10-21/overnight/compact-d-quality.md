# Compact D quality overlay

Prepared 21 October 2026 against `candidates/compact-a/`, using Skill Creator, `protocol.md`, `../proposal.md` and `coordinator-skill-review.md`. No article sources, review reports or held-out topic were read.

`candidates/d-quality/.agents/skills/` contains eight changed files only. Apply it over Compact A to obtain the effective seven-skill set; it is not an independently installable pack. Active skills are unchanged.

The overlay adds one short connected-explanation objective to writing: answer the reader's question through concrete mechanisms/examples, intelligible reasons and consequences, and sound choices. It asks for plain connected prose and removes decorative abstraction, stock transitions, repetitive templates/recaps and exceptions addressing only hypothetical reviewers. Necessary qualifications retain their causal or decision-making place. Revision and editorial wording support that distinction without a paragraph formula or banned-word catalogue.

Continuity now covers shared scenarios, entities, state, values and notation. The complete first-use audit remains mandatory, but its record captures decisions, fixes, prior-teaching dependencies and repair deltas rather than transcribing every correct definition. Relational table/key instructions are conditional. Table form depends on actual legibility; phone controls and their changing state or summary must remain understandable together, without prescribing sticky controls.

Assigned reviewers explicitly read only their own perspective file. Editorial, teaching and visual criteria receive small matching changes; their check names, counts and order are preserved. No review acceptance/schedule, orchestration policy, technical procedure or publication authority changes. The reference standard, evidence boundaries, fresh-worker rules and complete author reread/continuity pass remain.
