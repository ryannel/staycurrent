Static structure/link checks do not establish teaching quality.

The writing guidance is short and positive: connected explanations, concrete mechanisms, useful consequences and practical judgement; let the subject determine form/depth. Remove empty rhetorical habits while keeping necessary qualifications where they affect understanding. There is no banned-word list, paragraph formula, fixed word budget or separate untested model-specific skill pack.

## Reliability boundary

These are small transfer experiments on relational explanations, affine-coordinate/gesture teaching and latency plans. They can expose concrete failures and justify bounded process choices. They cannot establish that any arbitrary topic will be accurate, teach well or finish within a fixed time. Browser checks, illustrative arithmetic and injected event-policy tests retain their separate meanings. Physical touch/pen, cross-browser behaviour and the gesture download/save UI are not certified by the current evidence.

Publication is outside this commission. No draft articles will be pushed or made available as reading from this work.

## Bounded handoff clarification under test

The fresh repeat practitioner independently found the same reader-facing dependency gap as the first coordinate child: a working repository figure does not by itself deliver a usable implementation. Proposed F-03 adds only20words to figures: “For a promised runnable implementation, supply or link its setup and dependencies; verify it from the files the reader receives.” This applies when the article makes that promise; it does not turn every illustrative listing into a complete application. F-03 has4,908words total (24.8%below original). First repeat perspectives remain on frozen D-02; no inputs were silently changed during review. Prevention in a new cold first draft is not yet demonstrated by this clarification.
