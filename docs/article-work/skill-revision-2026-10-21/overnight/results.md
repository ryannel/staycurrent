# Compact writing and teaching skills: experiment results

Installed: **F-04**, seven skills and seven supporting files, **5,011 words versus 6,529 original: 23.3% less**. All required case checks, the fresh instruction audit and installation checks are complete. The count includes all supporting instructions. Source research and the requested changes remain in [the proposal](../proposal.md); methods and original records are in [the protocol](protocol.md).

The practical change is to spend review effort where it can change the result: review the complete provisional plan, repair coherent batches, confirm affected areas and retain independent complete technical and reader checks. The experiments rejected a cheaper policy that relied on focused checks alone. They also showed that a final editorial/teaching reading does not replace a second independent technical challenge.

The user's unseen authorisation-prompt wait and its associated queue are excluded from **all speed, process and quality assessments**. No tool or skill weakness is inferred from that pause. Consequently, the overnight elapsed clock is not an end-to-end production benchmark.

## What changes in the skills

| Skill | Final change |
| --- | --- |
| Orchestrate | Infer a useful commission from a bare topic; assemble a provisional recursive tree before routine whole-tree review. Use early node review only when an unresolved decision warrants it. One build/preview owner, frozen inputs, compact shared decisions/evidence. |
| Breakdown | Keep the small YAML format, meaningful article boundaries and concrete showing briefs. The commission determines useful depth; subtopics do not automatically earn pages. |
| Write | Keep the three complete reference articles. Ask for connected explanations, concrete mechanisms and practical judgement; remove stock rhetoric, decorative abstraction and qualifications that only answer hypothetical reviewers. No paragraph formula, banned-word list or fixed length. |
| Figures | Choose media by the understanding they provide. Keep actual phone/theme/control inspection and meaningful verification. Supply setup/dependencies when promising a runnable implementation. Load substantial stepper/database procedures only when relevant. |
| Continuity | Generalise the shared example beyond relational tables, retain complete first-use and downstream audits, record useful decisions rather than a transcript of correct uses. |
| Revise | Solve reader problems coherently, with restructuring when needed. Reread the whole article; reload reference passages and rerun checks in proportion to what changed and what earlier evidence no longer supports. |
| Review | Stable substantive acceptance; full first coverage, fresh affected confirmations, then separate complete technical and blind reader checks. Retain all five perspectives and 29 fixed checks. No round/time waiver for a required repair. |

The final complete practitioner has no earlier findings or repair history. The separate blind reader is editorial for articles and teaching for plans. Later local changes require fresh affected confirmations; repeat complete judgements when their overall applicability is invalidated. Corpus review remains required. A final complete assignment can also supply its corpus perspective only when the complete collection, exact version, allowed inputs and every check genuinely coincide; informed repair checks never substitute.

The late complete practitioner in the control found a false generalisation in unchanged native-histogram material that the first practitioner missed. Adding the final technical challenge to F then found a different defect: its promised class-separated comparison required data its measurement route never retained. Both discoveries changed the recommended policy. A subsequent value review also found that the control's expanded technical repair taught a schema taxonomy the classic-only example did not need; a coherent repair must preserve truth without turning into a detour.

## My own writing assessment

I read the complete resulting articles and current repairs myself, including captions, source panels and controls. The coordinate pair makes notation earn its place through one card's outward/return journey, then derives the retained-point reasoning and event lifecycle. The repeat child preserves that connected path. The relational repairs integrate previously detached transaction qualifications into the explanation.

I found no pervasive generic motivation, inflated abstractions, stock transitions, empty recaps or repeated rhetorical templates in the accepted current pieces. Necessary qualifications sit beside the decisions they change. Reviews did find local process-facing asides, an answered prediction and patch-like passages; those were repaired through reader effects, rather than a mechanical list of forbidden phrases. These judgements concern the actual samples, not every future output.

## Speed and reliability limits

Initial coordinate drafting phases were roughly seven minutes each; the repeat's draft/model phase was 4m54; the candidate's summed initial planner intervals were 8m25. **None is completed production time.** QA, source checks, build preparation, repair, review, coordination and records add work. The experiment itself also retained more snapshots and provenance than ordinary production needs; its directory volume is not a prescribed workflow.

The final pack was tested incrementally across these cases; there was no cold end-to-end production run of every final instruction together. The observations support avoiding duplicate node/whole-tree review, proportional repair checks and version-bound reuse of mechanical evidence. They do not establish an articles-per-hour guarantee, arbitrary-topic reliability or a Sol 6.1 versus Astra performance difference. Existing model settings were retained; no separate model packs are justified by these results. The logical final/corpus combination rule has no measured saving here: the coordinate run actually performed both editorial assignments, and its count remains thirteen.

Model arithmetic, injected adapter tests, current-browser observations and native database receipts remain distinct evidence. No physical touch/pen or cross-browser guarantee is made. Actual gesture save/download UI, direct-file execution and standalone light appearance remain unverified; exact linked HTML bytes were served unchanged for the recorded runtime checks. Article phone/light/dark checks are current.
