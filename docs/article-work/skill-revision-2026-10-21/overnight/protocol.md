## Quality judgement

Required: correct consequential claims; adequate evidence; terms and prerequisites arriving in time; concrete examples that reveal mechanisms and awkward cases; a coherent explanation; meaningful, readable and operable media; useful practical judgement within scope.

Personally inspect prose for inflated abstractions, stock transitions, repeated rhetorical templates, noun stacks, unsupported emphasis, gratuitous caveats, mechanical lists, repetitive recaps and passages that answer reviewers rather than readers. Judge the effect in context, rather than treating a banned-word count as writing quality. Preserve nuance and necessary caveats.

Adoption requires measured benefit without known substantive degradation, a compact skill set, independent assessment and transfer evidence. Record optional improvements and unresolved limitations. If the evidence does not establish reliability, report that and continue the most informative remaining experiment rather than declare success.
