## Expert-ability account

Recorded before reading the coverage card or existing article prose, 19 October 2026.

Predict table-lock waits and their queue effects; perform expand/backfill/validate/contract while old/new callers coexist; bound chunks and prove backfill completeness at a real invariant; build an index concurrently, reproduce an invalid index and cleanly recover; understand rewrite/default and NOT VALID/VALIDATE locks; use lock_timeout and evidence rather than assuming low-impact DDL means lock-free.

## Index lifecycle repair: independent ability account

Recorded 19 October 2026 before reading the expanded coverage card or the old
maintenance companion for this repair.

An operator can establish that an index is larger than its useful working data,
rather than inferring bloat from size alone. They need a comparable fresh baseline,
relation and index sizes, leaf density/empty/deleted-page evidence, the difference
between reusable gaps and bytes returned to the operating system, and the cost and
limits of physical inspection. They can decide whether a rebuild earns its CPU,
I/O, WAL, temporary disk and replica work, execute a concurrent rebuild with real
callers, verify validity and request semantics, and recover its stage-specific
leftovers without dropping a constraint's authority. They can assess an apparently
unused index across a representative interval, counter reset and read replicas;
identify uniqueness, foreign-key, order, collation, operator-class and predicate
duties before calling another index redundant; replace or remove it with an
explicit rollback option, execute the permitted concurrent drop and prove the
application request still behaves correctly. Native evidence must separate
continued caller progress from a universal claim that a concurrent operation never
blocks.

## Lifecycle repair reconciliation and handoff

The expanded RL63/RL119 card places the native lifecycle after concurrent build:
measure sparse versus reusable index space, rebuild under a stated operating
budget, then remove an access path only after accounting for its request and
constraint duties. The revised article keeps all original schema-change,
validation and expand/backfill/contract teaching. The new lifecycle sections are
contiguous before the data backfill, with a bridge back to representation change.
Generic operator selection remains RL112; heap retention remains RL111.

The old maintenance companion and its evidence were assessed. Its useful causal
shape survives: scattered versus range deletion, an equivalent fresh baseline,
physical inspection cost, rebuild resources and locks, failed-copy states,
representative usage and constraint duties, exact-definition comparison,
build/verify/drop and rollback capacity. Cross-engine operations stay outside this
native article. The existing small DeletedFigure is reused as Figure 2 beside the
new native measurements, with its different scale stated explicitly.

`evidence/rl119-lifecycle.py` and `rl119-lifecycle-observed.json` contain the new,
independent PostgreSQL 18.6 execution. Both full runs succeeded; the sealed second

with invalid-copy cleanup. `rl119-lifecycle-verification.md` records the final
results and boundaries. No earlier canonical evidence was changed.
