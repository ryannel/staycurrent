## Primary checks and new execution

Primary PostgreSQL 18 documentation was browsed on 19 October 2026; native statements/results were compared against the canonical JSON, not just author summaries.

- [HOT](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/storage-hot.html) and [index-only scans](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-index-only-scans.html): exact eligibility, original item routing, index coverage and heap visibility.
- [Routine vacuuming](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/routine-vacuuming.html): reuse/truncation/rewrite, wraparound, modern freeze flags and visibility.
- [Index types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-types.html), [BRIN](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/brin.html) and [multicolumn indexes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/indexes-multicolumn.html): operator relationships, physical summaries, maintenance and skip scan.
- [PREPARE](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-prepare.html) and [Using EXPLAIN](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/using-explain.html): five-custom estimated-cost rule, per-loop observations, inclusive work and sort evidence.
- [JSON operators](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/functions-json.html) and [constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-constraints.html): extraction on a wrong shape returns SQL null; CHECK permits unknown.
- [WAL configuration](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/wal-configuration.html), [WAL settings](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/runtime-config-wal.html) and [administration functions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/functions-admin.html): checkpoint redo boundaries, paused-target resume, acknowledgement boundaries and synced standby receive position.
- [PITR](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/continuous-archiving.html), [physical standby](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html) and [logical restrictions](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/logical-replication-restrictions.html): retained history, synchronous modes, missing DDL/sequence/large-object responsibilities.
- [ALTER TABLE](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-altertable.html), [CREATE INDEX](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-createindex.html) and [monitoring statistics](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/monitoring-stats.html): non-volatile default, concurrent-build remnants and named wait boundaries.
- [RLS](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-rowsecurity.html), [CREATE FUNCTION](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/sql-createfunction.html) and [pg_upgrade](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/pgupgrade.html): bypass/owner policies, secure temporary-schema ordering, creation/grant transaction and copy/link rollback resources. [Collation support](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/collation.html) bounds provider/version behavior.
