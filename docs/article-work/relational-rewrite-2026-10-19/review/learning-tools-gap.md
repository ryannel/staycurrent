# Learning tools: reopened collection review

19 October 2026, after delivery checkpoint `7562536`.

The user sampled the drafts and reported that they were not finding many
interactive learning tools like those in Indexes. That is a valid correction to
the acceptance judgement. **Collection status: repair required.** The earlier
article reviews remain evidence of what they checked, but do not establish that
the collection meets the commissioned teaching standard.

## What the sources show

[The inventory](learning-tools-inventory.json) covers all 54 draft sources. Six
articles embed retained Indexes or Storage components. There are 32 fixed stepped
figures, controlled by the shared renderer. They show successive authored states;
the reader cannot change the inputs or the schedule and obtain a new outcome.
SQL examples and downloadable native worksheets supply practical evidence and
reproduction, but leave most exploration outside the page.

Indexes does more. Its composite-index control changes column order and shows
entries re-sort, rows fetched and sorting work. Its covering-index controls change
included columns and visibility, then show which heap visits are still necessary.
Those are causal comparisons over a visible model. Merely revealing another text
panel does not provide the same learning experience.

The previous verification note's count of 31 generic figures was inaccurate;
the fresh source inventory finds 32 figures with the complete control set. That
correction does not change the central finding. A count of working controls
cannot establish that they provide the right learning support.

An independent bounded media audit reached the same collection-level judgement.
[Its findings](learning-tools-independent-review.md) distinguish useful static
examples from cases where a persistent changing model would do necessary work.

## Where production and review fell short

The commission preserved the reference articles and asked for causal figures and
meaningful variations. Its supplied Markdown shell made fixed text steps easy,
while only exposing existing Indexes and Storage components. We accepted that
available form too readily. The reviews resolved important comprehension and
technical gaps, and the browser checks established rendering and operability.
They did not adequately challenge whether the reader could see and explore the
mechanisms, as the reference and teaching-aid guidance require.

This was an implementation and acceptance failure, not an absence of instructions
in the skills. The authoring reference explicitly describes small simulations,
shared changing models and parameter controls chosen for a learning purpose.

## What the repair must accomplish

Audit each article for the relationships, sequences and variations the reader is
currently doing in their head. Use a visible model where it saves that effort,
and a control where changing something teaches a consequence. Static examples
remain suitable when they do the job. There is no figure quota and no requirement
that every article have an interactive tool.

Concrete candidates from the sampled pages are:

- **Joins (RL81):** add a second child record or move a predicate between ON and
  WHERE; show the matching pairs, output rows and changed total together.
- **Isolation (RL90):** advance two transactions under a chosen policy; keep their
  reads, pending changes and final capacity visible through the schedule.
- **Locks (RL91):** change acquisition order; show holders, a continuous wait span
  and the cycle or successful completion on a shared timeline.
- **Recovery (RL96):** move the recovery target across accepted bookings and a bad
  change; show restored state and the valid work still needing reconciliation.
- **Replication (RL97):** advance receipt and replay separately, then issue a read
  through a chosen route and show which committed state it can return.
- **Ranges (RL123):** change key layout or request distribution and expose the
  participating ranges and concentration of work.

These are proposed teaching jobs; component design remains to be done. The repair must
choose and build the aid from the actual argument, integrate it with the prose,
check its rules against evidence, and obtain independent isolated-media and
assembled-page review. A text stepper should be replaced or retained on that basis.
Native observations continue to support product claims; a browser model must keep
its scope explicit.

The implementation and media review remain outstanding. This addendum supersedes
the blanket collection readiness claim in the delivery handoff. Earlier source
hashes and per-article status entries identify the reviewed delivery snapshot;
they are not a new acceptance of its learning tools.
