# Product article learning tools

19 October 2026. Scope: RL110–132. This is an implementation handoff for the reopened media repair, not a readiness or publication decision.

The audit covered the opening mechanisms and later failure, maintenance and release cases of every owned page. Useful static code/results and native worksheets stay in place. The retained cache, covering-index, deleted-page, WAL-commit and checkpoint models already let readers change meaningful conditions. The repair replaces text-state reveals where the reader needed to retain records, boundaries or competing histories, and adds later comparisons where a native transcript alone left that work in their head.

The new tools live in `src/components/explainers/relational-product-tools/`. Named wrappers use one accessible result renderer; their rules and represented facts remain subject-specific. Tables hold exact records and decisions. Spatial jobs also have key intervals, page slots, replica votes, WAL rails, bucket runs or forked histories. Controls recompute outcomes from the selected conditions. All initial results are rendered by Astro; without JavaScript the record comparison and model bounds remain visible, and inactive controls stay hidden. There is no motion. Native selects, buttons and focusable table regions supply keyboard access. All colour treatment is scoped to the figure and includes light, dark and no-script system-theme fallbacks.

## Rules and evidence

The models use the exact article fixtures where their numbers matter. Small invented sets are labelled: eight heap slots, twenty-four BRIN times, the first thousand tag IDs, three historical timestamps, twelve scattered arrivals and illustrative replica placement. Native observed work counters remain in the article and are not replaced with invented timings.

The consequential rules were checked against the article's retained native records and current pinned primary documentation. Additional direct checks on 19 October 2026 covered [HOT](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/storage-hot.html), [vacuum and space reuse](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/routine-vacuuming.html), [standby acknowledgement](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html), [row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-rowsecurity.html), CockroachDB [distribution](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v25.4/architecture/distribution-layer), [survival goals](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v25.4/multiregion-survival-goals), [schema transitions](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v25.4/online-schema-changes) and [upgrade finalisation](https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs/v25.4/upgrade-cockroach-version). These are explanatory local models, not new native experiments.
