## Findings

### teaching-1 · medium · v02 native CockroachDB handoffs: r11 → r47, r28 → r46, r29 → r48

Problem: A reader can follow the finished introduction into the native read, performance or locality article without encountering **Keys, ranges and replication** (r44). The destinations then use the range/leaseholder model as something the reader can inspect, without first explaining it or directing this entry through its owner. The opening of r47 calls for distinguishing a busy range from a contested row; its hash-index section asks the reader to inspect ranges and their leaseholders. r46's route-verification section asks the reader to identify a range's leaseholder. r48 explains and accepts placement through per-range voters and the leaseholder. The general parents explain query work, snapshots or regional quorum, but do not supply CockroachDB's ordered ownership intervals and serving role.

Effect: The newcomer must supply a native architecture model or leave the advertised route to find it. They cannot yet interpret what a leaseholder location proves, why an index bucket is not a node/range, or what the observed range placement says about the requested policy. This is a prerequisite/order cost, even though a reader who already knows r44 can follow each destination well.

Suggestion: Before these native uses, give the missing minimal ownership explanation or clearly prepare the route through r44, allowing readers who already know it to continue. This can live in the parent handoff or the destination's entry/first relevant section. The existing r44 owner can carry the deeper account; the native children need not repeat the architecture article.

Evidence:

- **r00 → r19 → r21 → r29 → r28 → r46:** [r28, Why an old read can be safe](http://127.0.0.1:4560/review/relational-collection/distributed-reads/#why-an-old-read-can-be-safe) offers native follower-read modes. - **r00 → r19 → r21 → r29 → r48:** [r29, Home is a routing and access choice](http://127.0.0.1:4560/review/relational-collection/regional-placement/#home-is-a-routing-and-access-choice) offers the native settings.
