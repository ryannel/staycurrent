## Why work expands

The review skill requires initial four-perspective article checks, repair confirmation and separate complete practitioner/blind readings. Corpus policy then requires every perspective on the entire collection in every round, up to a third full round before focused-only handling. Unchanged judgements are too easily treated as stale because the version label changed. Mandatory fresh workers and repeated reference preparation add setup; complete per-piece matrices and duplicated evidence packets add coordination and reporting. Root has amplified these requirements instead of stopping to reassess their marginal value. A critical label for a false observed sentence should not imply collection-wide scope.

## Recommended smaller policy

Review each complete article once for technical accuracy, teaching/style and rendered behaviour, using the references where they help that judgement. Consolidate and repair actual reader problems. Confirm the change, its context and affected dependencies; keep unchanged checks. Widen only when a change invalidates a stated prior judgement.

Review the assembled collection once for coverage, ownership, duplication, prerequisites and routes, reusing article checks. Further work follows concrete findings, not a round counter or blanket final reading. Reuse a checker for confirmation when independence is no longer the purpose; reserve a fresh blind reading for a substantially rewritten argument or an unresolved quality question. Reports give findings, scope and material limits; they need not restate every pass at length.

Finish when accepted substantive findings are fixed and applicable checks cover the delivered version. Optional polish is not a delivery gate. For this collection, retain the completed coverage and confirmations, check the local37correction and its downstream meaning, then deliver with honest evidence limits. No third whole-collection pass is justified by that edit.
