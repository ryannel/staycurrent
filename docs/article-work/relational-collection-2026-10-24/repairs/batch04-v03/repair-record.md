## Decision and scope

The expiry authorisation point is the guarded UPDATE's predicate test **after the event row has already been locked by a separate SELECT FOR UPDATE in the same Read Committed transaction**. The lock remains through commit. The protocol does not promise that commit, receipt of its reply or an external action precedes the deadline. A wait on an unchanged row therefore occurs before the expiry test, and a competing generation cannot intervene after that test. Renewal, completion and release/rescheduling all use this procedure. Uncertain claims stay permanently retired; a fresh equality lookup cannot recover them.
