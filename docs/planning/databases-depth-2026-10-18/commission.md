## Derivation and independent challenge

Read `staycurrent-planning` and `staycurrent-style`, plus the authoring skill's
`references/depth-and-experiments.md`. Derive the assigned subject before opening
any existing collection outline, page list or coverage map. Do not read the previous
field proposal, its page tree, or other collections' derivations while deriving.

First save `<collection>-abilities.md`: audience and expert tasks; decompose each
task through the understanding needed to a teachable unit; include awkward cases,
operations and limits. Identify subject-specific tasks rather than fill a generic
matrix. A unit can need a sentence, worked cases, a mechanism investigation,
practical execution or external reference. Persist this before assigning pages.

Then save `<collection>-proposal.md`: derive the pages and sections from those
units. Each unit has one full teaching home or an explicit boundary/status. Give
each page a clear reader outcome, its owned units/sections, entry needs, teaching
form, necessary changed/failure case, and what a reader can decide afterwards.
Explain why each split earns another page and what was deliberately kept together.
No word/page quota; do not compress a product's internals, replication, recovery,
querying and operation into a nominal single product page.

Choose implementation depth because it enables understanding, not to match
another product's article count. Separate general teaching from actual product
mechanisms, commands and limits. Scope a coherent initial representative product
branch where the subject warrants it. Comparisons need credible alternatives and
conditions, not an exhaustive vendor list. Note any major product choice that
changes the collection's scope and needs discussion.

Browse primary sources to challenge consequential scope and implementation
assumptions. Record URLs and the distinction between a planning source and an
article claim needing later verification. Do not claim to have run experiments.
Shared distributed-systems teaching stays parked; explain database mechanisms
where necessary. Other planned fields remain separate. Shared teaching homes are
provisional until coordination; give direct entrants sufficient local context.

Independent plan reviewers will challenge missing ability chains, page cost,
overpacked implementation pages and reader entrances after these artifacts exist.
Do not start article writing or publish. Report files, derived counts, hard
boundary decisions and real uncertainty. Do not launch additional agents.
