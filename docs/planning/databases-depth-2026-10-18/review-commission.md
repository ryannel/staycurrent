# Independent challenge of the derived plans

Read the assigned subject names and audience first. Before opening the proposed
pages or the authors' ability lists, record what a practitioner must be able to
do, including awkward cases and the subject's limits. Persist that first account
in your review note, then read the assigned artifacts. Do not read the earlier
field plan, old inventories or other reviewers' findings for this first reading.

Audience: an intelligent application engineer new to the subject, with basic
programming and records. A collection should teach the expert-practitioner
understanding needed to make its stated decisions and operate its selected
implementation. It need not teach every product or an engine-implementation
curriculum. A direct entrant needs sufficient local context.

Use `staycurrent-planning` and the relevant independent-review guidance. A fresh
navigation reader instead uses only the supplied title tree and the context-free
voice reference. This review concerns planned teaching, not finished articles or
verified execution. Do not make article-readiness or publication claims.

Challenge the plan in both directions:

- Which necessary ability chain is missing, merely named or deferred behind an
  unjustifiably narrower scope? Name the consequence a reader would mishandle.
- Which proposed page promises more distinct investigations than its sections
  can coherently teach? Which split hides an essential final consequence?
- Which boundary costs another page without a distinct outcome? Propose a merge
  only when the worked explanation still teaches the necessary cases.
- Does product teaching expose actual mechanisms and useful practical work,
  while general teaching remains transferable? Do comparable product jobs get
  unequal attention without a subject reason?
- Can newcomers, selectors and incident readers enter at the right point? State
  any prerequisites you supplied yourself and any misleading title/promise.
- Where are shared mechanisms duplicated or handed to a page that does not
  actually own the required explanation? Distinguish a local reminder/application
  from full duplicate teaching.
- Are scope and deferred alternatives honest? Do not demand equal vendor counts,
  command encyclopaedias, a general distributed-systems programme or specialist
  material merely because it exists.

For each consequential finding, give the reader outcome, the exact proposed home
checked, why the collection owes it, and the smallest repair. Preserve useful
passages/boundaries and unresolved disagreements. Do not turn a concern into an
automatic new article. Prioritise consequences over votes and page counts.

Only write the assigned review note. Do not edit the authors' first artifacts or
the final coverage maps, start articles, change routes, commit, or spawn agents.
Report whether the plan is ready for user discussion or needs concrete repair,
with what that judgement can and cannot establish. A coordinator may supply a
later integration/repair overlay after your first findings are recorded.
