# Coordinating the independent depth derivations

18 October 2026. Work record for the revised plan, not article review or
publication approval. The previous proposal is recorded at Git revision
`1eac226`; it is the baseline to replace, not the outline for this derivation.

## Method

Each collection practitioner receives only the subject, audience, commission and
skills, in a fresh agent context. They are instructed not to read existing
inventories, previous maps or other derivations. Each saves its ability units
before allocating pages. The coordinator separately derives cross-field needs
and inventories old routes only for later reconciliation.

This makes the collection reasoning independent of the old article inventory.
It does not make any individual's list complete. Separate reviewers subsequently tested
missing abilities, unnecessary reader cost, overpacked pages, product boundaries
and entrances. Findings must change a concrete reader outcome or page boundary;
neither a larger count nor equal counts establish depth.

## Coordination rules

- Preserve the original ability and proposal artifacts, with later decisions
  recorded here and in the final coverage map. Do not rewrite a first impression
  to make it agree with the synthesis.
- Assign a shared mechanism once where its full explanation best serves its
  reader. Keep the context a direct entrant needs locally. A link to an unwritten
  page cannot stand in for a missing explanation in later production.
- Separate a family mechanism from a product's actual protocol, file structure,
  settings and procedures. Product depth may span a coherent branch. It must not
  become either one catch-all chapter or a complete vendor manual by default.
- Reconcile overlapping implementation branches by assigning their units to a
  single home. Redis's cache role and authoritative use are an expected join to
  inspect; analytical table publication and object publication are another.
- Treat selection, introduction, mechanism, diagnosis and operation as reader
  jobs. They can share a page where the explanation remains coherent; they are
  not compulsory navigation levels or a repeated template.
- Reconcile old reading only after deciding the new teaching homes. Record reuse,
  split/merge and uncovered needs without claiming that an existing paragraph
  already passes the revised brief. Keep current routes/status intact.
- Build the user-facing tree from the final allocation. Count introductions,
  family investigations and implementation investigations separately. Tools,
  aliases, assessments and deliberately deferred topics are distinct.

### Records review: accepted repairs and retained boundaries

[The first account and recheck](review-records.md) found six concrete issues.

1. General SQL concurrency was not enough to discharge native PostgreSQL
   competence. RL90, RL91 and RL93 now require actual server/driver concurrent
   reservations, absent-row/deadlock cases and lost replies, with statements,
   observations, errors and final state. RL110 owns the reusable native fixture.
2. CockroachDB schema jobs, release transitions and SQL-application migration
   were overpacked in RL130. Keep schema jobs there; add RL131 for upgrades and
   RL132 for application migration. RL74 is refined into three bounded units.
3. Cassandra query-table repair needed a native completion, not only a conceptual
   dual-write warning. W03 now requires an authoritative device ledger and derived
   alert table, interrupted writes, durable IDs, audit/rebuild and replay expiry.
4. The Redis join must preserve authoritative survival as well as cache behaviour.
   The explicit unit reconciliation and two entrances above resolve this.
5. Keep Redis A17 diagnosis and maintenance together provisionally, but name a
   distinct maintenance procedure and refusal/rollback gate. A11 owns initial
   setup; no later page may assume a hidden environment manual.
6. Cassandra C13 requires a bounded real local two-datacentre routing/isolation/
   return exercise. Real-region scale/latency is a separate optional exercise,
   not a way to make the required failure evidence optional.

The reviewer and coordinator retain a real boundary question over Cassandra C14
and C15. Separate recoverable file/history evidence from timestamp-aware useful
application restore now; revisit with the actual explanation. Removing the latter
consequence to make one short page would leave a missing ability.

### Storage review

[The first account and recheck](review-storage.md) supplied four accepted repairs.

- Cache validity includes revoked permission with unchanged bytes, tenant scope,
  missed invalidations and an explicit accepted-staleness/authentication policy.
- CA-A06/A13 must show a surviving business effect whose operation-ID evidence
  was lost by eviction, restart or promotion. Retention and survival of that
  evidence are part of the retry promise. The native counter/expiry case remains.
- CA-A11 owns a reusable pinned server/client, disposable data directory,
  persistence/memory/access settings, seeded keys and reset procedure.
- The Linux route starts with a disposable device, ordinary LVM mapping, XFS
  creation/mount and lifecycle/restart verification. Encryption variants are
  optional; no missing build/mount manual is assumed before filesystem internals.

Keep OB-S04 as one upload/read path through permissions/signing/keys/finalisation,
OB-S10 as incident evidence leading to a repair batch, FS-G06 as one coherent
concurrent-generation investigation, and FS-G07 as bounded path/identity/key
safety. These remain page-cost watchpoints, not automatic additional pages.

### Scope, counts and later commissions

The isolated proposals totalled 324 investigations, including the seven shared
pages. Remove three relational/shared duplicates, one keyed/shared duplicate and
nine duplicate Redis pages: 311. Split CockroachDB's three state machines instead
of keeping one: 313. The final allocation is 173 family/shared and 140 native
investigations. These counts describe the allocation; they do not prove expertise,
quality or a desirable production volume.

RDF/Jena now has real depth rather than a comparison-only boundary. Time-series,
shared tables and maintained SQL have distinct substantive routes. Bigtable,
Firestore/Couchbase, managed warehouses/Materialize operation, full GIS/scientific
arrays, offline synchronisation, Windows/SMB, full OpenZFS/Ceph and self-operated
object clusters remain explicit scope limits or possible later branches.

Versions, editions, licences, service access, lab capacity and reproducible fault/
restore procedures must be resolved before later article briefs promise native
execution. The three house articles calibrate explanation and media; they do not
supply a fixed outline. Existing reading is reconciled separately after these
choices, with no route/status changes and no automatic certification of old prose.
The plan is ready for the user's scope discussion. It commissions no articles.
