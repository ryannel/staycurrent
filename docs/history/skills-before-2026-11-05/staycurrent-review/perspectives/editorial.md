# Editorial perspective

**Inputs:** finished pieces as rendered; intended audience; relevant passages and finished figures from [Sequence diagrams](../../../../src/reference-articles/sequences.astro), [Indexes](../../../../src/reference-articles/indexes.astro) and [Storage and durability](../../../../src/reference-articles/storage.astro) to calibrate the judgement. Reviewers need not repeat the writer's complete-reference preparation. On a first editorial reading, do not read plans, earlier approvals or writer's notes; repair confirmation uses the accepted finding and affected context.

## Checks

1. **Titles and headings.** Follow the references in how they name articles and sections. Judge the actual wording in its section against reference examples. Prefer established, searchable industry terms where they fit naturally. Resolve doubtful wording in context; being able to infer its subject does not establish that it is well written.
2. **Plain sentences.** Plain, precise, connected prose; no compressed, decorative or writerly wording needing decoding.
3. **Terms.** Explain at first use and use consistently.
4. **Openings.** Give enough context to understand the question or claim and why it matters.
5. **Reader-facing text only.** Keep qualifications that prevent a mistaken reader inference or action. Keep planning, editing history and production bookkeeping in the work record.
6. **Prose and figures.** Remove diagrams and animations, including captions and controls, and read the complete prose. It must need no changes: no figure tours, playback instructions, undefined actors or conclusions whose premises existed only in the visual. Judge the explanation rather than just searching for figure references. Captions orient optional reinforcement; they do not supply missing article teaching. Keep useful text alternatives for the visual itself.
7. **One piece.** The article reads as if written in one go. Name passages that read as later patches: hypothetical objections, stacked conditions or interrupting asides. Necessary qualifications belong with the reasoning they change.

Judge whether the whole piece could sit beside the references in its selection, explanatory development and prose. Support that judgement with passages; an article-wide failure of the commissioned standard is substantive even when individual sentences are understandable.

In corpus mode, also check cross-article consistency of terms, names and running example.
