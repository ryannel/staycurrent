# Rendered checks

Judge the rendered figure in the article against comparable reference figures. For new or changed media, inspect it at 375 px and desktop, in light and dark; exercise every control and option, including keyboard use. A shared layout or player change needs checks across the affected figures. Retain applicable checks for unaffected media. Keep controls understandable with their changing state. Provide a text alternative for interactive content. Text contrast needs 4.5:1; large text and graphics need 3:1. Coordinate checks with the preview/build owner, or perform them directly in a standalone assignment, and record limits honestly.
